# Physics

Status: active ontology research focus.

Physics begins only after a semantic characteristic has been sealed and a
physical interpretation has been independently justified. A bare number does
not carry physical essence.

## Initial vocabulary

The first physical formation vocabulary is recorded in
[Basic Dimensions](basic-dimensions.md). It introduces

\[
\mathsf{BasicDimension}
:
\mathsf{PhysicalType}
\]

and the declared initial basis

\[
\mathcal B_{\mathrm{SI}}
=
\{
\mathsf{Length},
\mathsf{Mass},
\mathsf{Time},
\mathsf{ElectricCurrent},
\mathsf{ThermodynamicTemperature},
\mathsf{AmountOfSubstance},
\mathsf{LuminousIntensity}
\}.
\]

This is a conventional physical basis, not a claim that these seven dimensions
are metaphysically final.

## Quantity formation

The intended formation pattern is

\[
x:X_L,
\qquad
L:\mathsf{DimensionWord}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
xL:\mathsf{PhysicalQuantity}(L).
\]

The coordinate or scalar \(x\) receives its admissible type from the spatial
factor \(X_L\). The dimension word \(L\) is available only after the generating
open machine has been closed and its characteristic shown to be invariant in
the declared scope.

## Initial questions

1. Which closed semantic words admit a physical interpretation?
2. What distinguishes a coordinate, magnitude, dimension, unit, observable,
   and physical state?
3. Which chart changes preserve the physical quantity represented by \(xL\)?
4. Which proof shows that the characteristic word \(L\) is independent of the
   selected legal filling or presentation?
5. Which mathematical construction generates derived dimension words from a
   chosen basis?
6. Which residual records information forgotten by a physical observation?
7. When does a failed proof expose a missing physical structure rather than an
   implementation failure or bounded Unknown result?

No numerical sample, matrix representation, or familiar physical notation is
by itself evidence for physical semantics.
