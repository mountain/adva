# PH1: HolePolarityM6 Carries the Dimension L

Status: working physical hypothesis for circle-closing experiments.

## Statement

In this file,

\[
L
:=
\mathsf{Length}
:
\mathsf{BasicDimension}.
\]

The symbol \(L\) denotes the physical length dimension only. It does not
denote the left side of a surreal cut, a language, a linear lift, or an
untyped label.

Let

\[
\left(
\mathcal M_6^{\mathrm{hp}}
\right)^\partial
\]

be the open Three-Hole Polarity M6 Machine. Adopt the working hypothesis

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{CarriesDimension}_{\mathrm{work}}
\left(
\left(
\mathcal M_6^{\mathrm{hp}}
\right)^\partial,
L
\right).
}
\tag{PH1}
\]

The hypothesis attaches a provisional physical dimension annotation to the
open machine. It does not claim that the machine boundary, its three holes, or
the characteristic word \(L\) has already been closed.

## Experimental use

PH1 authorizes the circle-closing experiment to form a dimension-annotated
open machine

\[
\left(
\left(
\mathcal M_6^{\mathrm{hp}}
\right)^\partial;
L
\right)
:
\mathsf{DimensionAnnotatedOpenMachine}.
\]

For a residual coordinate with the required spatial type,

\[
x:X_L,
\]

the experiment may form the candidate physical quantity

\[
xL:
\mathsf{CandidatePhysicalQuantity}(L)
\]

and use it as the declared input to the reverse-polarity proof run:

\[
\operatorname{Run}_{-}
\left(
\left(
\mathcal M_6^{\mathrm{hp}}
\right)^\partial;
xL
\right).
\]

CandidatePhysicalQuantity is deliberately weaker than a certified
PhysicalQuantity. The latter requires a successful closure and semantic
formation certificate.

## Circle-closing result

The experimental operation must return one of

\[
\operatorname{CloseCircle}_{L}
\left(
\left(
\mathcal M_6^{\mathrm{hp}}
\right)^\partial;
xL
\right)
\Downarrow
\begin{cases}
\operatorname{Closed}(c_L,r_x,R),\\
\operatorname{Open}(g_L,R),\\
\operatorname{Unknown}(u_L,R).
\end{cases}
\]

Here:

- \(c_L\) certifies that the dimension assignment survives the checked
  closure;
- \(r_x\) is the typed answer residual;
- \(g_L\) records a structural obstruction;
- \(u_L\) records unresolved bounded execution; and
- \(R\) retains source, occurrence, polarity, filling, and proof history.

Only the Closed branch may support the promoted judgement

\[
\mathsf{CarriesDimension}
\left(
\mathcal M_6^{\mathrm{hp}},
L
\right)
[c_L,R].
\]

Open and Unknown do not refute PH1 automatically, but neither authorizes the
promoted judgement.

## Closure obligations

The first experiment must check symbolically that:

1. all three holes receive compatible typed roles under the \(L\) annotation;
2. the positive and negative threadings transport the same dimension word;
3. the designated positive output residual becomes a negative input variable
   without changing its physical type;
4. the reverse proof run returns a residual with a declared dimension;
5. no single-hole or double-hole polarity flip is used to repair a mismatch;
6. legal alternative fillings do not silently change the dimension;
7. source, occurrence, polarity, and residual ledgers survive closure; and
8. a closed result is independently replayable.

No numerical sample can discharge these obligations.

## Decisive obstructions

PH1 must remain open or be revised if:

- one of the three holes cannot be typed consistently with the other two;
- global polarity reversal transports \(L\) to a different dimension without a
  declared physical rule;
- different legal fillings produce incompatible dimension words;
- the answer residual has no stable physical type;
- closure requires deleting a source, occurrence, alternative filling, or
  prior residual;
- only sampled scalar agreement is available; or
- the machine closes syntactically while the semantic dimension judgement
  remains unformed.

## Nonclaims

PH1 does not assert:

- that HolePolarityM6 is already a closed machine;
- that \(L\) is already a sealed characteristic word generated by this
  machine;
- that the six-port incidence proof establishes physical length;
- that every port or hole separately carries length;
- that numerical values carry physical dimension;
- that the machine implements a physical law; or
- that the working hypothesis belongs in stable Adva semantics.

PH1 exists only to make the first exact circle-closing attempt well typed.
