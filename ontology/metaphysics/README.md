# Metaphysics

Status: foundational ontology vocabulary; deeper development deferred while
semantics, mathematics, and physics are the active focus.

## Primitive category-types

Introduce three primitive categories as types:

\[
\vdash
\mathrm{Quality}
:
\mathsf{OntologicalType},
\]

\[
\vdash
\mathrm{Quantity}
:
\mathsf{OntologicalType},
\]

\[
\vdash
\mathrm{Number}
:
\mathsf{OntologicalType}.
\]

The word category here records an ontological classification. It does not yet
assert the objects, arrows, composition, or laws of mathematical category
theory.

## Number-side terms

The Number side initially contains two distinguished terms:

\[
\vdash 0:\mathrm{Number},
\qquad
\vdash 1:\mathrm{Number}.
\]

No further structure is introduced here.

In particular, this declaration does not yet assert that:

- \(0\) is an additive identity;
- \(1\) is a multiplicative identity;
- \(0\ne1\);
- Number is the natural numbers, integers, reals, Booleans, or any completed
  number system;
- \(0\) and \(1\) denote falsehood and truth;
- \(0\) and \(1\) denote Null and Universal; or
- Quality, Quantity, and Number are reducible to one another.

Those relations, if required, belong to later semantic or mathematical rules
and must be proved in their declared scopes.
