# Mathematics

Status: active ontology research focus.

Mathematics develops typed constructions and proofs over semantic words whose
formation has already been authorized. It must not obtain a mathematical
object merely from the spelling of an ontological or physical symbol.

A minimal mathematical judgement has the schematic form

\[
\Sigma;\Gamma
\vdash_{\mathsf{Math}}
t:A
\;[\pi],
\]

where \(\Sigma\) is the available certified vocabulary, \(A\) is a declared
mathematical type, and \(\pi\) is the formation or proof certificate required
by the judgement.

## Initial questions

1. Which semantic words may enter mathematical formation rules?
2. How are equality, equivalence, duality, and isomorphism kept distinct?
3. Which natural-deduction rules consume certified machine results?
4. When can a proved construction be sealed as a new mathematical word?
5. Which residuals must remain available after abstraction?
6. Which composition theorem permits later programs to use the word without
   replaying its complete proof?

The metaphysical terms \(0\) and \(1\) are not yet arithmetic identities.
Addition, multiplication, order, induction, and number systems require
separate mathematical formation and proof rules.
