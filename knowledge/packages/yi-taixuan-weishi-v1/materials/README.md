## What the materials contribute

A finite observer answers only questions supported by its declared observation
policy. In the external mathematical model used here, a policy is a function
p:X->Y. It identifies a question about X only if that question is constant on
every fibre of p. This is a model for discussing finite discrimination; it is
not an implementation or identification of Adva's Rust observer type.

Yi supplies a small binary state space where operator composition, information
loss, alternative sampling policies and existential assumptions can all be
checked exhaustively. Taixuan supplies a ternary address space where coordinate
projection, carry arithmetic, line selection and definability can be separated.
The 47/34 cut needs four coordinates. This says what the specified projections
can distinguish, not why an editorial or historical boundary occurs there.

Weishi supplies a different kind of question: how are units produced by a
reading? Segment counts, verse labels, character overlap and sequence alignment
observe different properties. Their noninjectivity is demonstrable without
copying a text or pretending to formalize a doctrine. A common numeral supplies
no equivalence between an address space and a doctrinal enumeration. A lexical
alignment proposes a reading under a score; it is not a manuscript genealogy.

The Qin-Dayan comparison makes the source boundary especially clear. Residues
can uniquely determine a member of a finite interval even when a supplied
residue came from the wrong source. Reconstruction, source-to-observation
fidelity and independent checking are separate obligations. This is already
exhibited in the library; the packet acknowledges that prior result.

Discarding information may make a reported pattern stable simply because the
observer no longer distinguishes its changes. A deterministic coarsening cannot
restore a lost distinction. Whether discarding odd-order spectral terms improves
long-range prediction remains a separate empirical question requiring a defined
projection, retained residuals and held-out forecasts. None of these materials
establishes that forecast claim, assimilation method or physical interpretation.

The purpose of intake is therefore to retain justified continuations together
with their limits: a proof presentation, a finite witness, a counterexample, an
unresolved reading and a withdrawn explanation have different roles. No receipt,
hash, signature or green test collapses those roles into general truth.
