# ADR 0016: Keep typed open--close operations in a grounded research companion

- Status: accepted for bounded research V0
- Date: 2026-09-02

## Context

The bracket, through, triangle, connector, and distributivity calibrations all
preserve open boundaries, exact occurrences, multivalued relations, and
residual histories.  Their organization nevertheless began with
normalization or crossing around a candidate `Omega` singularity.

The revised hypothesis is more primitive: `{}[]()` presents three typed
apertures.  The empty syntax of an aperture is dual to its family of admissible
fillings.  Crossing and singularity should be derived from hole transport and
filling obstructions rather than used to define every hole.

Promoting `Hole`, `Open`, or `Close` directly into Rust would be premature.
Current data do not yet establish that function holes, cut ports, logic goals,
metavariables, observer charts, and singular points form one stable ontology.

## Decision

Add the nonauthoritative `adva.hole_research.HoleOpenCloseMachineV0`.

- One `TypedOpenHoleV0` is derived from an existing
  `CandidateThroughPresentationV0` with exactly one middle upper-wire
  coordinate.
- Existing through-relation elements are read as a finite observed filling
  fibre.  Their Rust-owned occurrence IDs, source IDs, copy paths, incidence
  indices, and middle wire remain unchanged.
- The existing interface domain selects the display surface `{}`, `[]`, or
  `()`.  The surface is a presentation, not a `ValueType` or intrinsic wire
  type.
- The one-compilation triangular artifact may be read as exactly one aperture
  per domain over one literal complete `ProgramSlice` residual.
- `close` selects one existing filling.  A multivalued fibre requires an
  explicit selector, an empty fibre is not closable, a wrong-domain selector
  is rejected, and residual erasure is an obstruction.
- `reopen` re-exposes the same aperture presentation but retains the selected
  filling and both close/open research trace events.
- All coordinates and trace ordinals are research execution data.  Python
  allocates no semantic hole, filling, witness, source, occurrence, wire,
  program, or certificate identity.

The implementation does not modify `adva.ir` version 1 or the Rust semantic
kernel.

## Consequences

- The three bracket forms receive one grounded hole-first interpretation over
  existing evidence.
- A grounded aperture with no observed filling remains distinct from absence
  of an aperture.
- A multivalued filling relation cannot be collapsed by an implicit choice.
- Closing is selection with retained alternatives and residual, not deletion.
- Reopening is not historical inversion even when the aperture state compares
  equal to its earlier presentation.
- No current result creates a new aperture type, extends the vocabulary,
  authorizes forgetting, identifies an aperture with `Omega`, defines active
  normalization, proves learning/proof self-duality, or establishes an open
  logic or universal computation.

## Promotion gate

A stable successor requires at least:

1. a decision separating or unifying program holes, observer apertures, and
   logical obligations;
2. Rust-owned identity and certificate types;
3. exact composition laws across adjacent slices;
4. explicit creation, filling, reopening, and forgetting obligations;
5. counterexamples for sharing, discard, empty fibres, multiple fillings,
   imported diagrams, and cross-program correspondence; and
6. a conservative-extension rule before any operation may create a new hole
   type or vocabulary.
