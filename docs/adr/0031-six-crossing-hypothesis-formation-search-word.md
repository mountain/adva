# ADR 0031: Form `search` only from a witnessed six-crossing hypothesis run

## Status

Accepted for one executable version-zero research experiment, subject to CI
replay. This does not change `adva.ir` version 1, the Lisp language, or the
three stable research mechanism labels.

## Context

The first magic-square experiment found a rich exact closure after 12,517
depth-first expansions. A later analysis exposed a much shorter description:
some normal order-four squares are generated by invertible linear maps on four
Boolean coordinates. The repository needs to test that proposed crossing
without rewriting the earlier search history, hard-coding one square as an
answer, or interpreting six observer-local names as six semantic mechanisms.

The finite-imagination target in ADR 0030 calls such a candidate a hypothesis
until a bounded program constructs and independently replays its witness. The
new vocabulary proposal is the verb `search`: it should enter the retained
boundary only when a run actually produces such a witness.

## Decision

1. Add the research method file
   `programs/bootstrap-0/hypothesis-formation.adva`. Its displayed name is
   `假设形成` and it is dispatched through the existing `learn` command.
2. Freeze the finite candidate family to the 20,160 members of `GL(4,2)`. A
   four-bit input is the row-major cell coordinate and the four output bits,
   plus one, give the cell value. Affine offsets are excluded.
3. Enumerate ordered nonzero row forms lexicographically, rejecting dependent
   rows. Partition the global ordinal by residue modulo six.
4. Attach the six residues, as presentation metadata, to all directed
   time-space-construction pairs in immediate opposite pairs:

   ```text
   run, compile, reveal, resume, name, instantiate
   ```

   The labels do not alter candidate identity or create six mechanisms.
5. Give every crossing exactly 3,360 candidate units of resource. One terminal
   run is either `witness` or `no_witness`; insufficient fuel is `suspended`
   and retains the same crossing and cursor.
6. Admit a candidate only if all of the following replay:
   - the four output forms are linearly independent;
   - four retained two-input XOR gates construct four distinct non-input
     output forms;
   - all ten line sums equal 34;
   - the exact characteristic-polynomial residual is one; and
   - the declared rotation/reflection/complement orbit has sixteen members.
7. In the frozen cost model, input basis forms and fanout are free and one XOR
   gate creates at most one new form. Four distinct non-input outputs therefore
   give a four-gate lower bound, and the retained trace realizes that bound.
8. On a witnessed run, form one fresh occurrence of the research-local verb
   `search` / `搜索`. Store its exact witness, crossing coordinate, definition,
   all method vocabulary, and the newly introduced word together. A result
   without a witness cannot form the word.
9. Persist every output through the common
   `(subject, method, object) -> (history, result, evidence)` interface. The
   transition retains the complete newly formed word and witness; the next
   frontier accumulates checked occurrence, word, and witness digest references
   without recopying the payload or identifying equal names and certificates.

## Consequences

The earlier 12,517-node history remains valid. The new experiment tests a
different, explicitly narrower hypothesis family and cost model. A short
linear construction may become a reusable word without becoming the unique
meaning of search, a fourth mechanism, or a stable CLI primitive.

Six residues cover the frozen finite family exactly. A negative residue result
is an exhaustive statement only about that shard and predicate. A positive
result may stop at its first witness and therefore does not enumerate the rest
of its shard.

The full payload/reference split is required for reuse: repeated frontiers must
not grow by embedding every predecessor witness again. Replay follows the
retained transition coordinate back to its complete word and certificate.

This experiment does not close the open `M6` semantic filler, derive the six
direction names from mathematics, prove a shortest path in another gate model,
claim a novel magic square, authenticate an observer, or show that six trials
solve arbitrary finite or infinite problems.
