# ADR 0012: Grounded multi-hole through candidates remain research relations

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-01

## Context

The exact triadic observer transition supplies occurrence-level opposite-pair
views and a complete `ProgramSlice`, while `GraftTrace` supplies ordered holes
and their exact entry wires.  The proposed through syntax

\[
\langle L\mid_N R\rangle=L\times_N R
\]

still lacked a grounded finite middle object.  Equal values, equal scalar
types, printed brackets, or a Python-created pairing cannot supply one.  Copy,
merge, discard, and source-free activity must remain visible.

## Decision

Add `MultiHoleThroughMachineV0` to the nonauthoritative `adva.research`
companion.  One experiment:

1. compiles one three-input PSC0 program once;
2. derives one Rust triadic transition and its graft trace from that same
   `KernelFunction`;
3. selects exactly one qualified call frame;
4. classifies every ordered frame hole by the unchanged lower-cut incidence
   on its exact entry wire;
5. follows exact Rust ancestry to frame exit incidences; and
6. explicitly quotients those upper incidences only by their exact upper
   cut-wire index.

For chart domains `D` and `E`, the candidate specializations are finite
relations

\[
q_D:F_D\rel N,
\qquad
q_E:F_E\rel N,
\]

and the through candidate contains precisely the pairs with a common middle
wire.  No value evaluation or newly allocated source, occurrence, wire, cut,
or event identity participates.

The adapter records ordered validation gates for Rust origin, interface typing,
ordered holes, same-diagram identity, the declared middle quotient, the fibre
product, residual retention, relational converse, exact adjacent carrier
composition, and the promotion boundary.  Failure blocks later gates but does
not erase the partial candidate or residual already obtained.

## Consequences

- One checked three-hole copy--merge fixture yields a two-valued relation from
  the space chart to two time occurrences over one construction-typed
  interface.
- A parallel two-output fixture has no common middle wire and is
  `not_representable`, even though both outputs have the same scalar type.
- A discard fixture produces a partial specialization instead of a permission
  to forget its occurrence or event.
- Relational duality is converse and involutive; it is not inverse execution.
- Exact adjacent composition is checked for the underlying Rust observer
  transition.  The adapter explicitly does not authorize composition of the
  derived through relations.
- The complete original `ProgramSlice`, including source-free activity outside
  the selected call frame, remains the residual.
- The result is bounded Python research evidence.  A stable through object,
  specialization, forgetting rule, normalization step, logic judgement, or
  universal-computation claim requires a separate Rust result and certificate.
