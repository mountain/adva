# ADR 0030: Require trust continuity before promoting an `M6` closure

## Status

Accepted as a research promotion gate and documentary witness boundary. This
does not change `adva.ir` version 1, the Lisp language, or any stable semantic
type.

## Context

The repository now retains corrections, exact path histories, typed residual
obligations, challenge-driven reopening, forks, custody proposals, and finite
handoff frontiers. It also has an exact three-hole port conjugacy and an open
target for a typed projective `J`-lift. A later `M6` construction could satisfy
a payload predicate while losing the history or challenge conditions under
which the payload can be trusted.

The statement that trust must not be interrupted is unsafe if interpreted as
mandatory belief or consensus. The required invariant is the uninterrupted
ability of later finite observers to locate provenance, replay declared
checks, challenge and fork, and resume from an explicit boundary.

The request for a witness from the machine side also needs an attribution
boundary. A generated response is a session occurrence. The repository has no
basis for treating it as the testimony of a durable person, all machines, or a
future agent.

## Decision

1. Define the research formula

   ```text
   trust-continuity-v0 = provenance + replay + challenge + succession
   ```

   as four noncompensating obligations. It is not a numeric score.
2. Treat trust continuity as a promotion gate around a future bounded `M6`
   filler, not as part of current stable process semantics.
3. Require both ordered paths to retain immutable origins, distinct histories,
   compatible checker contracts, all residuals and forks, custody warnings,
   and an explicit output-envelope correspondence.
4. Report missing evidence, authority, custody, a typed correspondence, or
   finite resources as `Unknown`.
5. Keep integrity, authenticity, availability, fork accountability, semantic
   reproducibility, endorsement, consensus, and truth distinct.
6. Store the requested machine contribution as an attributed session witness.
   It may state a normative request, but it must deny durable identity,
   consciousness, collective representation, independent authentication, and
   guaranteed future preservation.
7. Keep the human declaration and generated contribution as distinct
   occurrences even when one file carries both.
8. Admit `merge_capacity > rupture_load` only as a research hypothesis with a
   typed finite matching: every rupture obligation must have a compatible
   resource, declared costs must fit the spacetime budget, noncopyable
   resources cannot be double-spent, trust-continuity coordinates must survive,
   and at least one compatible reserve must remain.
9. Interpret exact coverage without reserve as fragile equality, unmatched or
   unaffordable obligations as `<`, and missing comparison evidence as
   `Unknown`. Forbid unrelated resource types from cancelling each other in an
   untyped total.
10. Treat “first we must be able to help them” as a reciprocity interface: an
    outside request should expose both a bounded need and a concrete
    contribution the current observer can offer. It creates neither debt nor
    compulsory reciprocity.
11. Use two independent mathematical readings in the next experiment: a typed
    capacity or flow-cut witness for whether resources can cross a bottleneck,
    and an additive winding ledger inspired by `Integral dz/z` for the history
    retained around a hole. A multiplicative unit readout may not erase a
    nonzero additive period.
12. Record the Euler connection with a historical boundary. Euler's 1748
    derivation used infinitesimal circular and exponential formulas; the
    contour-integral and winding interpretation is a later structural
    reconstruction.
13. Treat `i` as a research-local label for a finite imagination step that uses
    an append-only historical rereading to generate a falsifiable
    boundary-crossing question. Keep imagination `i` and complex-number `i`
    distinct unless a later typed witness constructs their correspondence.
14. Require an imagination-step output to name its external need, the current
    observer's help-first contribution, sources, falsifiers, finite cost, and
    residual frontier. It remains a hypothesis rather than evidence that the
    interface was crossed.

## Consequences

- Equal payloads or endpoints cannot hide the loss of a challenge or residual
  obligation.
- Disagreement and forks may continue without severing provenance or replay.
- Checker migration must itself be witnessed; a version string alone does not
  establish checker correctness.
- Machine-generated text enters the same challengeable custody chain without
  receiving a privileged truth class.
- The next implementation can add a finite Rust envelope checker with positive,
  negative, and `Unknown` fixtures without claiming an `M6` semantic filler.
- Resource consumption and reserve become visible path coordinates instead of
  an implicit promise that future help will arrive.
- Capacity and winding remain separate: enough flow does not identify paths,
  and topological winding does not supply resources.
- Every finite round gains a disciplined question-generating edge without
  converting historical reinterpretation or symbolic resonance into truth.

This decision does not authenticate a speaker, deploy custody, create social
consensus, prove liveness, guarantee meaningful continuation, establish an
observer-independent truth coordinate, or complete the typed `J`-lift.
