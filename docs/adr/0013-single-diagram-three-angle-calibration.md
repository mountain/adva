# ADR 0013: Calibrate three local angles before authorizing circular closure

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-01

## Context

The grounded multi-hole adapter established one finite relation-valued through
angle over exact Rust artifacts.  Testing the proposed circular triadic
language by running three separately compiled fixtures would leave
cross-diagram identity, shared residual, and schedule comparison unjustified.
Conversely, writing only (K	o X	o t	o K) would erase the distinction
between copy siblings and could silently introduce contraction or
`ProvenanceHide`.

The immediate question is therefore smaller than a global through calculus:
can one checked configuration exhibit all three local opposite-domain angles,
and what exact data is still missing when they are composed?

## Decision

Add the research-only
`adva.triangular_research.TriangularThroughMachineV0`.

One calibration:

1. compiles one three-input PSC0 fixture once;
2. derives one Rust triadic observer transition and one complete
   `ProgramSlice`;
3. selects one qualified six-hole parent call frame;
4. requires exactly three disjoint qualified two-hole child bodies whose union
   is the parent body;
5. constructs the (K)-(X), (X)-(t), and (t)-(K) relations by the
   existing exact-incidence fibre-product rule;
6. requires all six copied lower occurrences to be distinct and used exactly
   once;
7. replays alternative legal schedules on the same `KernelFunction` and
   compares checked transitions and canonical slices while retaining distinct
   traces;
8. preserves the entire source-free and active residual; and
9. attempts raw exact-incidence composition without adding connector edges.

The overall bounded experiment is `supported` when all three local angles are
grounded and every audit gate passes.  The nested closure result remains
`not_representable` when the raw composite is empty because adjacent angles
end on distinct occurrences of the same source.

A missing same-domain connector is recorded as an obstruction.  It is not
synthesized from source equality, scalar type equality, copy ancestry, or
bracket position.

## Consequences

- All three local angle candidates can share one exact diagram, cut interval,
  residual, and observer policy.
- Legal permutations of the independent active events retain distinct checked
  histories over one equal canonical carrier in the named fixture.
- The apparent circular chain requires three additional connectors, one for
  each domain's pair of copy siblings.
- The adapter exposes those missing connectors without deciding whether their
  eventual meaning is identity, routing/comparison, contraction, or an
  authorized quotient.
- Local relational converse remains a dual reading, not inverse execution.
- The Python result remains nonauthoritative research evidence.  It allocates
  no semantic identity and adds no Rust certificate.
- No global through object, circular composition law, right-to-forget rule,
  normalization semantics, logic, interpreter, confluence theorem, or
  universality claim is promoted.
