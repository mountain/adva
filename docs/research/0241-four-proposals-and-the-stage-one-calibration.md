## 2. What proposal 4 (the geometry foundation) must contain

- The **passive spot floor** `theta_sun * L` at every declared distance, and the
  statement that a *penumbra diameter* is `D + theta_sun(full size) * L`, which is why
  the floor's constant pairing is the full angular size.
- The **étendue ceiling** `B * lambda^2` per mode, which is what forces power into free
  space and keeps fibres for phase only.
- The **usable fraction**, with the erratum of note 0240 corrected rather than carried:
  the Earth's angular radius must be paired with the Sun's angular **radius**, giving an
  occulted fraction of about **0.851** and a usable fraction of about **0.149** at the
  second Lagrange point, in place of the reported 0.787.
- The **pattern scale**: the usable light-dark geometry sits at about `theta_sun * L`
  (3.7 km near Earth, 334 km geostationary, 13 800 km at L2), with finer structure washed
  out by the extended source and coarser interference structure requiring sub-coherence
  apertures.
- The **symmetry constraints**: which point groups may be realised by a constellation,
  and which of them sit inside the rotation subgroup of the relevant spacetime symmetry
  group; the declared reading is that a polyhedral point group is a subgroup of SO(3),
  hence of the Lorentz group's rotation part, while the time direction enters as a
  declared schedule invariant under the corresponding time translations.
- The **work-region and work-time calculus**: given the constellation, which regions can
  be addressed, at which times, with which duty cycle, and with what geometric floor.
- The **ablation energy-level arrangement**: the declared reading is that the energy
  levels used to distribute tasks are matched to the ablation levels of the target
  medium, so that a task is assigned to a level rather than to a platform.
- **High safety, topology preservation, controllable human impact, bounded error and
  tolerance of that error** as declared obligations, each with a falsifiable counterpart.

## 3. Stage one: calibration, then continuous learning

- **Stage one is calibration**, not control: large amounts of observational material are
  **computed, aggregated and distributed**, inside the **December–January** window
  already fixed for the earlier chain.
- Then the programme **continuously observes and learns the evolution of the small
  perturbations** it creates, so that **learning enters from stage one** rather than
  after a control phase.
- As more satellites are launched the network grows into a **large, complex learning and
  regulation network**.
- The two key invariants are **not breaking the overall topology** and **continuous
  learning**; both are obligations to be declared with falsifiable counterparts, not
  slogans.
