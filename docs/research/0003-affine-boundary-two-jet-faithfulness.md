# Marked affine boundary two-jet faithfulness

Status: bounded theorem and executable calibration
Date: 2026-08-29
Depends on: `0002-affine-exp-observer-spectrum.md`
Executable witness: `tests/python/test_affine_exp_observer_spectrum.py`

## Purpose

The preceding experiment identified the exponential ray generated by

\[
  y=\log x,
  \qquad
  q=e^{-y}=x^{-1}
\]

as a dual observer carrier for positive affine programs.  The remaining
geometric question is whether that carrier is merely a coordinate choice or
is the displayed form of a neighborhood selected by the process itself.

For an affine state map

\[
  D_{m,a}(x)=mx+a,
  \qquad m>0,
\]

the point at infinity is invariant after projective completion.  Its ideal
\(I_\infty=(q)\) and formal neighborhood

\[
  \widehat{\mathcal O}_{\mathbf P^1,F_\infty}
  \simeq \mathbb R[[q]]
\]

are therefore preserved by pullback.  The geometry selects the filtered
carrier; the marked AM chart \(q=1/x\) presents it by coefficients.

## Theorem: faithfulness through the marked second jet

Let \(J^2_\infty=\mathbb R[[q]]/(q^3)\).  Pullback by \(D_{m,a}\) sends

\[
  q\longmapsto
  D_{m,a}^*q
  =\frac{q}{m+aq}
  =m^{-1}q-am^{-2}q^2+O(q^3).
\]

Write the first two coefficients as

\[
  D_{m,a}^*q=c_1q+c_2q^2+O(q^3).
\]

Then, relative to the marked parameter \(q=1/x\),

\[
  \boxed{m=c_1^{-1},\qquad a=-c_2c_1^{-2}}.
\]

Consequently, the homomorphism from the positive affine fragment to
automorphisms of its marked second-jet carrier is faithful.  In particular:

1. the first graded piece \(I_\infty/I_\infty^2\) detects the multiplicative
   scale \(m\);
2. the extension from first to second order detects the additive parameter
   \(a\);
3. after the affine hypothesis is declared, these two coefficients determine
   the entire germ

   \[
     D_{m,a}^*q
     =\sum_{k\ge1}(-1)^{k-1}a^{k-1}m^{-k}q^k.
   \]

The result is not an eigenvalue theorem.  The scale appears on the associated
graded carrier through weights \(m^{-k}\), whereas addition is encoded by the
non-split, degree-raising part of the filtered action.

## Contravariant composition

For

\[
  D_1(x)=m_1x+a_1,
  \qquad
  D_2(x)=m_2x+a_2,
\]

state composition gives

\[
  D_2\circ D_1
  =D_{m_2m_1,\,m_2a_1+a_2}.
\]

In the basis \((q,q^2)\), with columns recording pulled-back basis elements,
the marked second-jet table is

\[
  C(m,a)=
  \begin{pmatrix}
    m^{-1}&0\\
    -am^{-2}&m^{-2}
  \end{pmatrix}.
\]

Observer pullback reverses state composition, and the executable experiment
checks

\[
  C(m_2m_1,m_2a_1+a_2)
  =C(m_1,a_1)C(m_2,a_2).
\]

Thus the matrix-like object is a derived coordinate table for the action on
the jet carrier.  It is neither a primitive program nor a Cartesian
state-space ontology.

## Chart covariance

The recovery formula above is marked-chart faithful, not unmarked-chart
invariant.  Let a second adapted parameter be

\[
  \widetilde q=h(q)=uq+vq^2+O(q^3),
  \qquad u\ne0,
\]

and suppose a germ is displayed in the old parameter by

\[
  f(q)=c_1q+c_2q^2+O(q^3).
\]

Conjugating the germ by the chart change gives

\[
  \widetilde f
  =h\circ f\circ h^{-1}
  =c_1\widetilde q+
  \left(
    \frac{c_2}{u}
    +\frac{v(c_1^2-c_1)}{u^2}
  \right)\widetilde q^2
  +O(\widetilde q^3).
\]

Hence \(c_1\), the action on the conormal line, is invariant, but \(c_2\)
is not an absolute scalar.  Equivalently, with

\[
  C=
  \begin{pmatrix}c_1&0\\c_2&c_1^2\end{pmatrix},
  \qquad
  H=
  \begin{pmatrix}u&0\\v&u^2\end{pmatrix},
\]

the two displays satisfy

\[
  \widetilde C=H^{-1}CH.
\]

The intrinsic unmarked object is therefore the filtered formal-neighborhood
action up to adapted-chart conjugacy.  Recovering the literal affine
translation parameter additionally requires the marked AM chart, its unit,
and the associated decoder.

## Neighboring no-go

Second-jet faithfulness fails immediately outside the affine hypothesis.  The
non-affine positive map

\[
  R_b(x)=x+\frac{b}{x},
  \qquad b>0,
\]

fixes the same projective boundary fiber and induces

\[
  R_b^*q
  =\frac{q}{1+bq^2}
  =q-bq^3+O(q^5).
\]

Its second jet is identical to the identity, although its third-order term is
nonzero.  Thus the valid conclusion is exactly:

> The marked second jet is faithful on the declared affine fragment; it is
> not a complete invariant of general arithmetic programs preserving the
> same boundary fiber.

This counterexample is deliberately checked through the same Adva compilation
and validation path as the affine programs.

## Relation to the two spectral manifestations

The calculation supports a bounded form of the proposed space-time duality:

\[
  \boxed{
  \text{invariant boundary fiber}
  \longrightarrow
  \text{ideal/jet filtration}
  \longrightarrow
  \text{contravariant filtered action}
  \longrightarrow
  \text{graded weights + extension data}
  }.
\]

The spatial manifestation is the selected fiber together with its formal
neighborhood.  The temporal manifestation is the process acting
contravariantly on that neighborhood.  Choosing \(q=e^{-y}\) displays the same
carrier as an exponential-polynomial ray, and choosing the jet basis displays
the action as a triangular matrix.  Neither display creates the carrier.

What lifts automatically from an invariant fiber is the ideal filtration and
its conormal/jet tower.  A distinguished dual vector or numerical translation
coordinate does not lift automatically: those require a trivialization such
as the marked AM chart.

## Scope

The executable claim is restricted to checked finite Adva diagrams over
positive `Real` inputs, the projective boundary fiber at infinity, the marked
chart \(q=1/x\), and formal series through the declared order.  SymPy computes
coefficients after Rust has compiled and validated the program.  No generic
pullback, jet, spectrum, formal-completion, or objectification API is added to
Adva.

The next geometric extension should transport this construction between the
two projective boundary charts, treating the neighborhoods of zero and
infinity as objects of a formal-neighborhood groupoid.  Only after the
transition law and residual order are explicit should the work attempt a
chart-independent spectral stack or an objectification mechanism.
