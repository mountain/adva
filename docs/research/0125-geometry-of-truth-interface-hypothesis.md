Status: human-proposed working hypothesis and interface method; documentary
vocabulary, with the coherence laws and physical interpretation still open.

## 2. The supplied interface principle

The shared connection between physics and mathematics is **scale**: people
supply measurement, obtaining mathematical values from the physical world.
Mathematics corresponds to **form**. Logic corresponds to **language**, and
people supply **names**. These are proposed as the working method and
principles of our interface.

| Aspect | Place in the proposed interface | Human contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical truth | Physical phenomena encountered through measurement | Performing and situating measurement |
| Mathematical truth | Values, forms, and relations articulated mathematically | Establishing the scale and interpretation used in the measurement |
| Logical truth | Statements and inferential relations expressed in language | Supplying names and making the intended reference explicit |

Scale mediates the physics--mathematics connection. Form and language retain
their different roles. The hypothesis asks for coherence among these roles;
it does not assume that their meanings can be exchanged without conditions.

## 3. Continuity with the preceding hypothesis

Mingli's preceding proposal places arithmetic truth and physical truth in
relation across the interface of form and matter, and attributes interface
fluctuations to that positioning. It also gives physical truth a proposed
geometric meaning called **空虚**, rendered provisionally as **emptiness**.

This note preserves those as research hypotheses. The generative mechanism
of the fluctuations and the exact geometric meaning of emptiness are not yet
defined. No identification with a numerical zero, a physical vacuum, an empty
set, or the absence of outstanding proof obligations is supplied here.

## 4. A finite working method

The following is an operational elaboration for experiments, rather than an
additional physical law:

1. State what is measured, with its scale, units or reference, protocol,
   resolution, and available uncertainty information.
2. State the mathematical form used to express those observations or the
   finite problem, retaining its domain and assumptions.
3. State the proposition, its names, their referents, and the inference or
   verification rule under which the statement is assessed.
4. Retain witnesses for each admitted connection. Keep observation records,
   formal certificates, interpretation choices, and unresolved differences
   accessible in their respective roles.
5. Reuse a name with its definition and scope. A change of meaning should
   retain the earlier reference and the reason for the revision.

A name can organize a question before the question is solved. Measurement
supplies an interface to physical events; formal verification supplies a
judgment within a declared mathematical scope. The coherence sought here
requires explicit connections between them. A successful finite calculation
alone does not establish the proposed physical account.

## 5. Immediate calibration through the existing search experiments

The bounded sorting-network experiment in
[PR #68](https://github.com/mountain/adva/pull/68) gives a practical place to
apply this method:

| Aspect | Concrete record |
| --- | --- |
| Measurement and scale | Runtime and memory observations with a declared machine, clock, units, and measurement boundary |
| Mathematical form | A finite layered comparator network, its binary input domain, and the residual set of inputs it fails to sort |
| Language and names | A proposition such as "this network sorts every binary input of the declared channel count," together with the named search programs, source version, and checker |
| Witnessed connection | A retained network, exact verification result, reproducible execution description, and separately identified resource measurements |

An empty residual set has a precise finite meaning in this example: all
binary inputs in the declared domain pass. Its possible relation to the
hypothesized geometric emptiness remains a separate question.

The LABS experiment in [PR #66](https://github.com/mountain/adva/pull/66)
provides another instance: signed sequences, exact autocorrelations, and a
specified energy threshold. Success is assessed against that threshold;
the total energy is not required to vanish. Resource measurements and claims
of search advantage remain separately testable.

These experiments make the interface method usable without requiring the
whole hypothesis to be settled first. They can test whether preserving
measurement, form, language, and their links helps another person or agent
state a problem, reuse a witness, or detect a failed interpretation.

## 6. Open obligations and engineering boundary

The next formal work must specify what objects and maps support coherence,
what they preserve, and which comparison or transport law a witness proves.
It must also explain what counts as failure of a proposed connection.

Physics/mathematics/logic is not assigned automatically to time/space/
construction or compute/verify/learn. Such assignments would require their
own typed interpretations. Existing semantic authority, dependency order,
M6 obligations, and immutable inquiry artifacts remain as documented.
This note introduces no new axiom into the checker and claims no completed
physical theory or general equivalence of the three kinds of truth.
