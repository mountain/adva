## 3. Native topology of the seven faces

Read as cells, the seven nonempty subsets of \(D\) are the nonempty faces of a
two-simplex:

- \(K,X,t\) are vertices;
- \(KX,Xt,tK\) are edges; and
- \(KXt\) is the two-cell.

The six proper nonempty faces form the boundary

\[
\partial\Delta^2\simeq S^1,
\]

while adjoining \(KXt\) fills it:

\[
\partial\Delta^2\cup\{KXt\}
=
\Delta^2\simeq D^2.
\]

Thus \(KXt\) is natively a filler, not a seventh isolated point at the centre of
a hexagon. The \(A_2\) centre-plus-six-modes picture may remain a spectral
quotient candidate, but it does not preserve this face incidence by itself.

Three increasingly strong statements must remain distinct:

\[
\gamma(0)=\gamma(1),
\qquad
\operatorname{Hol}(\gamma)x=x,
\qquad
[\gamma]=0\in\pi_1(X).
\]

They mean, respectively, closed path, fixed action, and null-homotopic path.
Only the last statement supplies a topological filling.

Pairwise edges do not manufacture the triple cell. Any proposed \(KXt\) closure
must retain a two-cell or higher-coherence witness.

## 4. Backward search does not yet define a first cause

For a forward operation \(F_d\), backward evolution is generally the relation

\[
\operatorname{Pred}_d(y)
=
\{x:F_d(x)=y\},
\]

not an inverse function. It may branch, cycle, have infinite descending
chains, or approach an invariant limit set rather than an endpoint.

The following notions are distinct:

1. no predecessor under one rule;
2. a root of one represented history;
3. a common ancestor of three projected histories;
4. an observer-relative certified root;
5. a universal initial cause.

A finite search that finds no predecessor does not certify an empty predecessor
fibre. Timeout, fuel exhaustion, and an unresolved frontier remain evidence,
not negation.

A candidate common-origin halt therefore needs at least

\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{OriginHalt}_Q(M;r)
\Longrightarrow{}&
\bigwedge_{d\in D}\operatorname{RootCert}_{d,Q}(r_d)\\
&\land
\operatorname{GlueCert}_Q(r_K,r_X,r_t;r)\\
&\land
\operatorname{Quiescent}_Q(M).
\end{aligned}
\]

Even this is observer relative and may reopen under refinement.

## 7. Relativity challenges the \(E^3\)-plus-time picture

Ordinary Euclidean \(E^3\) is contractible, complete, and boundaryless, but not
compact. A constructionally "closed \(E^3\)" must not be called topologically
compact without a separate compactification.

Relativity supplies no observer-independent decomposition in which absolute
space is closed first and time fibres are attached afterwards. A global
splitting

\[
M\simeq\mathbb R\times\Sigma
\]

requires strong causal hypotheses such as global hyperbolicity and is not a
canonical preferred foliation.

At an event \(p\), the future unit timelike directions form

\[
\mathcal O_p
=
\{u\in T_pM:g(u,u)=-1,\ u\text{ future}\}
\simeq H^3.
\]

Its ideal boundary is the sphere of null directions,

\[
\partial H^3\simeq S^2.
\]

This gives a serious candidate interpretation: three remaining degrees may be
the three boost or rapidity coordinates of one observer fibre, or the three
ontology views may define three sections of a common observer bundle. Neither
interpretation has been derived from Adva.

Pairwise comparison would then use Lorentz transformations

\[
\Lambda_{KX},\Lambda_{Xt},\Lambda_{tK}\in SO^+(1,3).
\]

Their product around a triangle need not be the identity. An accounted
nontrivial product may encode velocity-space rotation or spacetime curvature.
Forcing it to identity would erase valid relativistic information. Only an
undefined, untracked, or lossy transport is a leakage witness.

Causal order can determine at most conformal metric information under suitable
distinguishing hypotheses. Scale, volume, proper time, energy, and the
stress-energy/curvature dynamics remain missing.

## 12. Natural units as a graded quotient and physics as a constraint-moduli problem

Natural units do not turn physical laws into untyped pure arithmetic. They
choose a scale convention in a space of dimensioned quantities. The SI itself
is defined through fixed numerical values of defining constants; changing unit
systems changes numerical coordinates while quantity equations remain the
primary invariant objects. See the [BIPM SI
overview](https://www.bipm.org/en/measurement-units) and the dimensioned-algebra
formulation in [arXiv:2108.08703](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.08703).

Let \(\Delta\) be a dimension lattice, provisionally

\[
\Delta
=
\mathbb Z\langle M,L,T,\Theta,Q,\ldots\rangle,
\]

and let arithmetic-expression presentations carry a grade

\[
\deg:\mathcal E_{\mathrm{AEG}}\longrightarrow\Delta.
\]

Multiplication adds grades. Addition is defined only between compatible
grades. Constants carry nonzero grades, for example

\[
[c]=LT^{-1},
\qquad
[\hbar]=ML^2T^{-1},
\qquad
[G]=L^3M^{-1}T^{-2},
\qquad
[k_B]=ML^2T^{-2}\Theta^{-1}.
\]

Choosing \(c=\hbar=G=k_B=1\) imposes relations on the dimension lattice. If
\(\Delta_{\mathrm{const}}\) is generated by the selected constant grades, the
natural-unit grading is schematically

\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{nat}}
=
\Delta/\Delta_{\mathrm{const}}.
\]

This is a quotient of the grading or a gauge choice of unit coordinates. It is
not a quotient by physical dynamics, and it does not remove dimensionless
couplings, mass ratios, boundary conditions, or measured data.

Let a physical theory be presented by a typed family of constraint expressions

\[
\Phi=(\Phi_\alpha)_\alpha.
\]

Before naturalization, each equation must be dimensionally homogeneous. Its
expression-level solution locus is

\[
\mathcal Z_{\mathcal E}(\Phi)
=
\{e\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{AEG}}:
\Phi_\alpha(e)=0\ \text{for every }\alpha\}.
\]

The resulting physical classification problem has the schematic form

\[
\mathcal M_\Phi
=
\mathcal Z_{\mathcal E}(\Phi)
\big/
\left(
G_{\mathrm{units}},
G_{\mathrm{gauge}},
\sim_{\mathrm{presentation}}
\right).
\]

The three quotients in this display are not identified:

- \(G_{\mathrm{units}}\) changes quantity coordinates;
- \(G_{\mathrm{gauge}}\) changes physically redundant field presentations; and
- \(\sim_{\mathrm{presentation}}\) changes arithmetic or program syntax.

A correct construction must state their actions, commutation or interchange
laws, residuals, and order of quotienting.

For example, the special-relativistic mass-shell equation

\[
E^2-c^2\mathbf p^2-m^2c^4=0
\]

becomes

\[
E^2-\mathbf p^2-m^2=0
\]

when \(c=1\). In expression space this is a typed quadric constraint, not a
proof that energy, momentum, and mass are the same physical quantity. Restoring
\(c\) is a lift from the natural-unit presentation to a dimensioned
presentation.

The lift must retain at least

\[
(\text{dimension ledger},
\text{constant calibration},
\text{observer},
\text{boundary conditions},
\text{source/history residual}).
\]

Otherwise naturalization creates another leakage: two expressions coincide in
the quotient but cannot be reconstructed as the same measured physical
quantity.

Field equations expose a second boundary. Equations such as

\[
i\hbar\partial_t\psi=H\psi
\]

or Einstein's equation are not scalar arithmetic constraints. They require
operator, tensor, derivative, field, domain, boundary, and regularity types.
The current arithmetic-expression carrier can encode only a finite symbolic
presentation of such equations. It does not yet carry their analytic solution
spaces.

The resulting AEG research problem can therefore be stated precisely:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{minipage}{0.82\linewidth}
Given a dimension-graded expression space, a scale action, and a typed family
of physical constraints, construct the invariant moduli of satisfying
presentations and a residual-preserving lift back to dimensioned,
observer-indexed physical data.
\end{minipage}
}
\]

Equivalently, the characteristic problem is to find a scale- and
presentation-stable feature

\[
\chi_\Phi:
\mathcal Z_{\mathcal E}(\Phi)
\longrightarrow
\mathcal C_\Phi
\]

that is invariant under admitted unit changes, separates declared physical
observables, and retains enough fibre data for reconstruction.

This translation is a valid mathematical problem shape. It does not establish
that every physical theory has such an AEG presentation, that the quotient is
well behaved, or that its characteristic is complete.

## 13. Conservative conclusion

The ontology round revealed a potentially coherent architecture:

\[
\text{triadic program faces}
\to
\text{topological fillings and fibres}
\to
\text{causal candidates}
\to
\text{possible physical realization}.
\]

It did not establish any composite arrow in this chain.

The most important surviving insight is not a proposed spacetime formula. It
is the discipline that a visible invariant may sit above an unclosed,
branching, or history-bearing fibre. A program may look stationary while its
lift remains open. This phenomenon connects relative halt, residual evidence,
observer refinement, and the current caution around physical time.

The correct next move is therefore not to close the ontology by terminology.
It is to make the missing maps executable one at a time.
