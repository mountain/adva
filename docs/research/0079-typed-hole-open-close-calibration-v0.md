# Typed Holes Before Singularities: Open--Close Calibration V0

Status: bounded research proposal with an executable Python companion over
unchanged Rust-owned artifacts.  This note records the exploration path as
part of the evidence.  The path matters because a discarded question or
failed identification may later expose a new typed hole.

## 1. Why this note changes the order of foundations

The recent line of work began with crossings around an `Omega` singularity.
That emphasis was productive: it separated local through relations from
global closure, exposed multiplicity, and forced residual process history to
remain visible.  It was nevertheless one step too late.

The new observation is:

> A finite observer does not begin at a closed state.  Its beginning is
> already an aperture.  The empty bracket is not an empty object placed at the
> aperture; the bracket itself is a typed hole.

Consequently, the dependency order should be tested as

\[
\boxed{
\text{typed hole}
\longrightarrow
\text{filling fibre}
\longrightarrow
\text{transport or crossing}
\longrightarrow
\text{normalization}
\longrightarrow
\text{singularity and reopening}
}.
\]

An `Omega` point is then not the definition of every hole.  It is a candidate
name for a locus where the present finite observer cannot choose, represent,
or glue the filling data coherently.  Every such singularity involves an
aperture, but an ordinary typed input hole need not be singular.

## 2. Exploration path retained

### 2.1 Finite representation and the three-domain boundary

The original problem was how a finite observer, bounded in construction,
space, and time, can express an open world.  Logic suggested one partial
answer: a finite syntax may have open interpretation only by abstracting and
forgetting details.  The triadic programme proposed a finite `3-form` in which
each vertex reads a characteristic of its opposite pair.

The bracket surface

```text
{}[]()
```

was first tested as a normal form for mixed construction, space, and time
brackets.  The finite Raw111 experiment established bounded normalization and
seven stable partial/full domain outcomes.  It did not establish an open
logic or universal computation.

### 2.2 Boundary rather than machine state

The next correction was that `{}[]()` is not the whole machine state.  It is a
shared typed boundary retained before and after a run.  Programs are processes
on that boundary.  Braids then supplied a useful reversible skeleton and a
holonomy distinction:

\[
R^3=I
\quad\text{at the coarse coordinate level, while}\quad
\widetilde R^{,3}=\Delta^2
\]

may retain process history.  Pure braids were nevertheless ruled out as a
complete computing language because the checked programme contains
noninvertible copy, discard, merge, and idempotent behaviour.

### 2.3 Through syntax and the singularity programme

The surreal-style form

\[
\langle L\mid R\rangle
\]

was proposed as a syntax for crossing a boundary.  The engineering form became
a relation or span, not an ordered pair of closed values:

\[
L\longleftarrow C_d\longrightarrow R.
\]

The multi-hole through adapter, single-diagram triangle, and connector
trichotomy then established several bounded facts:

- a local through form may be genuinely multivalued;
- relation converse is not reverse execution;
- three local angles need not have a global circular composite;
- direct sibling comparison and source quotient are different mechanisms;
- a source quotient may fabricate a coarse closure that exact occurrences do
  not possess; and
- retaining a residual does not by itself authorize forgetting.

These results motivated geometric comparisons with nodal fibres, knots,
figure-eight crossings, `(p,q)` periodicity, hyperbolic compactification, and
the AEG programme.  Those comparisons remain research hypotheses at their
declared scopes.  In particular, the existence of three domains does not by
itself force an embedding into physical three-space.

### 2.4 Learning, proof, and the finite-observer obstruction

The distributivity calibration showed that learning and proof can share one
bounded characteristic extractor:

\[
a(x+y)\longrightarrow ax+ay\longleftarrow ax+ay.
\]

It also retained the unequal copy, occurrence, constant, discard, graft, and
history residuals.  The result supports a common finite characteristic kernel;
it does not prove a full contravariant equivalence of learning and proving.

The philosophical obstruction then became explicit.  Empirical learning can
only range over a previously available hypothesis language.  Fixed-language
logical completeness does not explain the birth of a new characteristic or
new vocabulary.

The following historical calibrations sharpened the gap:

- Kant's determining judgement applies a given universal, while reflective
  judgement seeks a universal from particulars;
- productive imagination mediates what can be presented and schematized;
- a Hintikka open saturated branch supplies model evidence inside a fixed
  language;
- a Henkin construction adds witnesses under syntactic obligations but does
  not automatically invent arbitrary new concepts.

Thus imagination cannot be modeled merely as another normalization in a fixed
grammar.  At minimum it must be allowed to propose a new typed question,
coordinate, chart, or aperture, with conservative-extension obligations.

### 2.5 Empty and universal as two readings of one interface

Placing `{}[]()` on a circle exposed an inside/outside duality.  The apparently
symmetric empty/universal pair becomes directed only after a finite observer
chooses a process cut.  A finite run has relative open boundaries

\[
P:\partial^-P\rightsquigarrow\partial^+P,
\]

not an absolute creation point and final end.  Semantic reversal of the
boundary does not erase historical direction, schedule, energy, or residual.

The new hole reading makes the duality precise in a finite syntax:

\[
\operatorname{Syntax}(h_d)
\simeq \text{minimal content},
\qquad
\operatorname{Fill}(h_d)
= \text{all admissible fillings visible in the declared carrier}.
\]

The hole is syntactically empty and semantically a family.  This is the
current conservative meaning of the empty/universal duality.  It does not say
that the finite observed filling fibre is the metaphysical universal or a
proper class.

## 3. Candidate primitives

Write

\[
h_K=\{\},\qquad h_X=[],\qquad h_t=().
\]

These are three typed apertures, not three copies of one monoidal unit.  The
candidate operations are

\[
\operatorname{Open}_d,
\operatorname{Close}_d,
\qquad d\in\{K,X,t\}.
\]

Their first operational readings are:

| Domain | Opening candidate | Closing candidate |
|---|---|---|
| construction `K` | expose a variable, witness position, constructor, or new question | select or construct a term/witness |
| space `X` | refine an observer, distinction, or chart | project or identify under a declared observation |
| time `t` | continue, branch, or allocate further search | finish one bounded trace or commit one continuation |

This table is a proposal, not a stable operation registry.  In particular,
creating a previously unavailable hole type is stronger than reopening one
already grounded aperture.

## 4. Open and close are not installed as inverses

The immediate red-team law is

\[
\operatorname{Close}_d\operatorname{Open}_d\ne I,
\qquad
\operatorname{Open}_d\operatorname{Close}_d\ne I
\]

unless an additional theorem states exactly which state, history, and
observation are compared.

Closing records at least:

- the typed aperture;
- the selected filling;
- every unselected alternative;
- the complete process residual;
- the authority or obligation under which the selection was made; and
- a close trace event.

Reopening may recover the same aperture presentation, but it must retain the
released filling and the earlier close event.  It is therefore a state-level
re-exposure, not historical inversion.

For a multivalued fibre, an unqualified close is refused:

\[
|\operatorname{Fill}(h)|>1
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Close}(h)
=\texttt{NotRepresentable}
\]

until an explicit selection witness is supplied.  For an empty filling fibre,
the aperture may still exist; it is the close operation that is not
representable.  This separates

```text
no aperture
```

from

```text
a grounded aperture with no observed filling.
```

## 5. Relation to learning, proof, and imagination

The aperture vocabulary suggests four distinct operations:

| Reading | Hole action |
|---|---|
| experience | supplies fillings to already expressible holes |
| proof | closes declared obligations with checked witnesses, or refutes every candidate in a finite scope |
| learning | abstracts concrete fillings into a reusable typed aperture while retaining residual differences |
| imagination | proposes a new hole shape, type, chart, or connection not present in the current language |

Learning can therefore close some instance detail while opening a feature
position.  The same characteristic algorithm may support proof and learning
as opposite-polarity readings, but their histories and obligations are not
simply inverse.

The current V0 tests only selection and reopening of apertures already grounded
by checked carriers.  It does not implement imagination, vocabulary birth,
reflective judgement, Hintikka saturation, Henkin witness generation, or a
complete open logic.

## 6. Re-reading existing validation through apertures

1. **Bracket normalization.**  A normal bracket surface places apertures at a
   calibrated boundary; it need not eliminate them.  Finite enumeration is
   completeness inside one chart, not open-world completeness.
2. **ProgramSlice.**  A slice is a finite process between two open cuts.  Its
   lower cut is a relative beginning with exposed ports, not absolute nothing.
3. **Multi-hole through relation.**  The relation is a finite observed filling
   fibre for an interface aperture.  Multiplicity is possible continuation,
   not noise.
4. **Triangle.**  Three local interface apertures may share one residual even
   when there is no global filling that closes the circle.
5. **Connector trichotomy.**  Comparing two retained fillings differs from
   quotienting them.  Quotienting needs a right-to-forget judgment.
6. **Distributivity.**  A common polynomial feature is one filled observer
   aperture.  It does not erase the process residual or create a program
   equation cell.

This interpretation preserves all earlier negative controls.  It changes the
organizing primitive, not the checked Rust evidence.

## 7. Executable calibration V0

The research-only module `adva.hole_research` consumes existing
`CandidateThroughPresentationV0` values.  It does not change Rust, `adva.ir`
version 1, or the meaning of any existing artifact.

### 7.1 Grounding

One typed aperture contains:

- the existing interface domain;
- the bracket surface used only as a presentation;
- the existing graft frame coordinate;
- exactly one existing middle upper-wire coordinate in V0;
- the complete finite relation elements read as possible fillings;
- the exact occurrence IDs, source IDs, and paths of each endpoint; and
- the unchanged complete `ProgramSlice` residual.

The tuple `(frame, domain, middle wire)` is explicitly a research coordinate,
not a new `HoleId` accepted by Rust.

### 7.2 Experiments

The first suite checks:

1. the one-compilation triangular carrier exposes exactly one typed aperture
   of each domain and prints `{}[]()`;
2. all three apertures share one literal `ProgramSlice` residual;
3. a one-element filling fibre closes without an extra selector;
4. reopening recovers the aperture presentation while retaining close/open
   trace and released filling;
5. the two-element copy--merge fibre refuses an unwitnessed close;
6. selecting one of those two fillings retains the other as an explicit
   alternative;
7. a wrong-domain close and a request to erase the residual are different
   failures; and
8. the discard fixture yields a grounded but unfillable aperture rather than
   erasing the aperture or provenance.

No scalar evaluation participates.

## 8. What this experiment can and cannot support

### Supported if the suite passes

- Existing ordered holes and relation-valued through carriers admit one
  coherent finite aperture presentation.
- The three local interface types can be displayed as `{}[]()` over one exact
  residual.
- Close can be calibrated as explicit finite selection rather than deletion.
- Reopen can recover an aperture state without pretending to invert history.
- Multivalued and empty filling fibres remain distinct typed situations.

### Conservative conclusion

The current exact program carrier is sufficient to test a hole-first
organization without changing stable semantics.  It is not yet sufficient to
define a general open logic.  In particular, the V0 filling fibre is finite,
fixture-relative, and already present in the checked relation.

### Red-team boundaries

- A function input, cut port, syntax metavariable, logical obligation, and
  singular point are not automatically the same kind of hole.
- The bracket surface is not a stable `ValueType` or intrinsic wire label.
- One relation element is not an executable residual program.
- Selecting a filling does not authorize contraction, provenance hiding, or
  source quotient.
- Reopening does not recover forgotten information; V0 forbids forgetting.
- An empty observed filling fibre does not prove that no filling exists in an
  enlarged language or observer.
- A finite filling set is not the universal class of all future fillings.
- No current operation creates a new hole type or validates imagination.
- No aperture is identified with `Omega` merely because it is open,
  multivalued, or presently unfillable.
- Passing three unique close tests does not produce a global circular
  execution or logic completeness theorem.

## 9. Next questions opened by the calibration

1. What common interface distinguishes an input port, observer aperture,
   logical goal, existential witness hole, and concept-forming hole without
   conflating them?
2. Which operation changes only a filling and which changes the hole type or
   boundary grammar?
3. What certificate gives a finite observer the right to close a spatial
   aperture by quotient rather than by retained comparison?
4. Can `ObservationallyEqual`, `NotRepresentable`, and `FuelExhausted` be
   refined into explicit requests to reopen `X`, `K`, and `t`, respectively,
   without treating every failure as language extension?
5. What conservative-extension square is required when imagination proposes a
   new construction hole?
6. When do locally fillable apertures admit a compatible global section, and
   when does failure define an `Omega`-type singularity?
7. Does a full cycle of open/close polarities have nontrivial monodromy or
   holonomy after exact histories are retained?
8. Can energy or irreversible resource use select a historical direction even
   when the aperture geometry has an empty/universal dual reading?

The immediate engineering conclusion is narrow:

> Preserve apertures, filling fibres, selections, alternatives, residuals,
> and traces as different data.  Do not promote singularity, forgetting,
> imagination, or open-logic completeness until separate finite evidence
> forces the corresponding types and certificates.
