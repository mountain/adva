## 1. What the run establishes exactly

- **Constellation family.** The declared point group `T_h` of order 24 is generated by exact
  integer closure; the twenty declared vertices split into orbits of **8** and **12**; there
  are exactly **10 antipodal pairs**; inversion is present with determinants `−1` on exactly
  twelve elements; the element orders are `1, 2, 3, 6` with **no five-fold element**, so the
  group is crystallographic; and every element maps the vertex set onto itself, so a general
  pyritohedron moves only within each orbit. The declared alternative, the snub cube, has
  group `O` of order 24 with **zero** improper elements — chiral — and orders `{1,2,3,4}`, so
  it too is crystallographic; its orbit has 24 vertices and no antipodal pair.
- **Two regions.** Ten sunward and ten anti-sunward members, one from each antipodal pair, with
  the sunward indicator exactly odd under the pairing element and the two addressings exact
  negatives, in a four-step table computed in advance.
- **Spacetime-group conditions.** An invariant pair gives transport **0** with per-step flows
  `−15/8, 15/8, 15/8, −15/8`, produced as an executed rejection of the claim that it transports;
  the pairing set has order 24 with the pairing element carrying a half-period shift, and it is
  non-symmorphic in the declared sense. A broken pair gives **−15/2** at one gradient sign and
  **+15/2** at the other, with the exact sum zero — the reversal is exact, and a second broken
  pair reverses exactly as well.
- **Schedule compatibility.** An invariant schedule gives a standing pattern with zero
  transport; a schedule carrying a phase gradient gives a travelling pattern with non-zero
  transport whose direction follows the gradient's sign.
- **Entry window W1–W4.** Declared threshold `1/2`, declared latent heat `6`, declared
  connected region `24`, declared fraction `1/96`, declared bound `1/4`, declared action `1/8`,
  with the exact inequality `1/8 < 1/96 · (6 · 4) = 1/4`; four refusals executed: entry below
  the threshold, entry after sealing, a silent threshold crossing, and an action reaching the
  bound.
- **Ablation levels and stage one.** Eight declared tasks on five declared ordinal levels in
  counts `2, 2, 1, 1, 2`, with platform routing and an out-of-set level both refused and a
  magnitude audit clean; stage one carries 4096 declared units with identical counts and index
  sums at all three stages, launches 1..8 with a cumulative network `1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 21, 28,
  36`, learning units entering at the first step, and both the topology-broken and
  learning-delayed controls refused.
- **Overreach controls, all refused.** An icosahedral constellation (order 60, 24 five-fold
  elements) is lattice-incompatible; the pattern's shape does not fix the direction (the
  standing case transports nothing); the two orbits are **combinatorially different**, so the

  the wrong way round and the run refuses the true-false direction instead; both family
  members share the declared combinatorial type (`20 − 30 + 12 = 2`, `24 − 36 + 14 = 2`); and
  the full `T_h` is not inside `SO(3)`, with twelve determinant `−1` elements as the witness.

## 3. The disclosed defect

**The transport in this run is not a genuine measure-carrying flow.** The declared weight is
exact-**even** in its two endpoints, so the declared uniform measure is **not** exactly
stationary: the exact vertex divergence is non-zero (`−3/2, −1/2, −3/4, −1/4, ...`) and the
payload states `the_declared_uniform_measure_is_exactly_stationary: false`. The zero and the
exact reversal are therefore exact and checkable, but they rest on an **edge sum with a
declared residual**, not on a doubly-stochastic transfer of the kind executed in note 0242.
The contract's mechanism sentence is consequently only **partially** realised here, and the
measure-carrying zero remains the one in `0242`. Two further disclosures: the failed control
above is a genuine failure and not a wording artefact, and the checker was **rewritten late**
in the session after an over-broad line-wrapping pass corrupted the first version; the current
file reproduces the same verified facts and numbers, and the payload's `checker_sha256`
matches it. The run's own report states all three rather than leaving them to be discovered.
