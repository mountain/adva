# Research 0121: Six-crossing hypothesis formation and the word `search`

## Question

Can the opposite side of the retained time-space-construction presentation
turn the long magic-square discovery trace into a shorter witnessed method?
If a finite crossing succeeds, can the witness and every word used to read it
remain together as a new verb without changing the three-mechanism core?

The program is named `hypothesis-formation` / `假设形成`. Its claim is
falsifiable and narrow:

> At least one of six directed finite shards contains an exact normal
> order-four magic square generated by a minimum four-XOR linear bijection in
> the declared cost model.

## One mechanism, six readings

Every invocation is still one `learn` edge with the common interface:

| direction | slot 1 | slot 2 | slot 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| input | `subject` frontier | `method` hypothesis formation | `object` finite fuel |
| output | `history` trace | `result` witness or negative/suspension | `evidence` checks and next frontier |

The six off-diagonal domain names are ordered in opposite pairs:

| shard | directed reading | local name |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 0 | construction to time | `run` |
| 1 | time to construction | `compile` |
| 2 | time to space | `reveal` |
| 3 | space to time | `resume` |
| 4 | space to construction | `name` |
| 5 | construction to space | `instantiate` |

These names index disjoint schedules. They are not candidate identities,
intrinsic wire types, or evidence that one direction has the proposed real-world
meaning.

## Candidate space and exact witness

Write the input cell coordinate as four bits

```text
x = (column-low, column-high, row-low, row-high).
```

The method enumerates every invertible binary matrix `A` and reads

```text
value(x) = 1 + binary-value(A x).
```

There are exactly

```text
|GL(4,2)| = (16-1)(16-2)(16-4)(16-8) = 20160
```

such maps. Global candidate ordinal modulo six places 3,360 candidates in each
shard. A full six-run boundary therefore covers the declared candidate family
without overlap.

The construction witness starts with basis forms `0001, 0010, 0100, 1000`.
Every retained gate XORs two already available forms and must create a fresh
one. All four output forms are distinct and absent from the input basis. Since
one gate can add at most one form, at least four gates are necessary. A
four-gate replay proves optimality only in this exact model.

The resulting sixteen values are checked independently as a normal magic
square: all ten line residuals are zero, the exact characteristic-polynomial
residual is one, and the existing three transformations generate sixteen
distinct members.

## Six bounded runs

Each run receives the full 3,360-unit shard budget. The expected executable
observations are:

| run | name | state | examined | first global ordinal | output forms |
| ---: | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | `run` | witness | 1,279 | 7,668 | `6,11,14,9` |
| 2 | `compile` | witness | 1,976 | 11,851 | `9,13,7,6` |
| 3 | `reveal` | witness | 1,324 | 7,940 | `6,14,11,9` |
| 4 | `resume` | witness | 1,248 | 7,485 | `6,9,14,11` |
| 5 | `name` | no witness | 3,360 | none | none |
| 6 | `instantiate` | witness | 1,208 | 7,247 | `6,7,9,13` |

Thus the initial existential hypothesis is witnessed five times in five
different shard occurrences. The `name` shard is fully exhausted under this
partition; that is a finite negative result, not an execution error and not a
global impossibility theorem.

The first witnessed square is

```text
 1 11  8 14
 6 16  3  9
15  5 10  4
12  2 13  7
```

The retained generator uses output forms `(6,11,14,9)`. Each form is a Boolean
linear expression in the four coordinate inputs; the serialized witness stores
the exact four-gate dependency trace rather than relying on this display.

## Word formation

A positive run packages one fresh word occurrence with:

- local name `search`, display name `搜索`, lexical class `verb`;
- the directed crossing coordinate and both candidate ordinals;
- the complete four-XOR and magic-square witness;
- the witness digest;
- the method's complete retained vocabulary; and
- `search` as the sole introduced vocabulary item.

After six terminal runs the final frontier contains checked references to five
distinct `search` occurrences. Each positive transition retains its complete
word, vocabulary, and witness exactly once; later frontiers carry occurrence,
word-digest, and witness-digest coordinates instead of embedding the payload
again. They share a spelling and definition but do not thereby become one
occurrence or one semantic identity. The fifth run contributes a retained
negative history and no word.

`search` also remains below the three mechanism labels. The transition that
forms it is `learn`; no fourth stable mechanism or standalone `adva search`
command is introduced.

## Reproduction

Start from the three committed inputs and feed each next frontier to the same
method and resource:

```console
cargo run -p adva-witness --bin adva -- \
  learn \
  programs/bootstrap-0/hypothesis-formation-frontier.adva \
  programs/bootstrap-0/hypothesis-formation.adva \
  programs/bootstrap-0/hypothesis-formation-resource.adva \
  --output target/hypothesis-formation-1.adva \
  --frontier-output target/hypothesis-formation-frontier-1.adva
```

Repeat for frontiers 1 through 5. Tests also exercise insufficient fuel,
method drift, witness tampering, the six terminal states, and final vocabulary
retention.

## Boundary

This experiment establishes one local compression from a large explicit
permutation problem to a small exact linear chart. It does not establish that
the chart was historically used by the earlier depth-first run, that the
direction labels caused the solutions, or that the shard with no witness is
intrinsically obstructed. Changing enumeration, sharding, affine policy, gate
set, fanout cost, or target predicate changes the question and requires a new
version.

Most importantly, five arithmetic witnesses do not close the repository's
open `M6` semantic relation or the external truth fiber. They form a reusable
research word inside a declared finite boundary; they do not prove that every
future search crosses its boundary in six runs.
