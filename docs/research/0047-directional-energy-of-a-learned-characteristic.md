# Directional Energy of a Learned Characteristic on a Finite 3-Form

Status: exploratory finite calibration extending
[`0045-logic-as-learned-characteristic.md`](0045-logic-as-learned-characteristic.md)
and
[`0046-proposal-for-logic-on-a-3-form.md`](0046-proposal-for-logic-on-a-3-form.md).

The executable calibration is
[`tests/python/test_triadic_characteristic_energy.py`](../../tests/python/test_triadic_characteristic_energy.py).

The previous notes studied how a finite observer extracts a characteristic,
forgets detail, and obtains a logical entailment order.  One major structure
was absent: **energy**.  A characteristic says what distinction remains stable
under a family of observations.  It does not say how strongly the distinction
varies along temporal, spatial, or constructive moves, how much work is needed
to create or maintain it, or which direction a relaxation process would take.

This note adds the smallest exact energy layer that does not overstate its
physical meaning.  It uses a finite cubical observation carrier and computes
three directional Dirichlet energies.  The result is an energy **vector**

\[
\mathbf E_Q(f)
=
(E_t(f),E_X(f),E_K(f)),
\]

not an uncalibrated scalar.  A total energy exists only after positive
conversion weights between the three domains have been declared.

The central finite result is an exact observer-forgetting decomposition.  If
the constructive coordinate is forgotten by averaging each finite fibre,
then the feature splits into a coarse part and a zero-mean residual, and all
three directional energies split exactly.  For a parity characteristic, the
coarse feature becomes the constant value \(1/2\); all of its directional
energy survives only in the residual.  A premature Booleanization would erase
this fact.

This is a mathematical energy calibration, not a physical energy theory.  It
adds no stable energy, metric, observer, feature, learning, or dynamics API;
does not modify `claims.toml`; and does not change the active `ProgramSlice`
priority or Rust semantic authority.  Python owns only the exact finite
research fixture.

---

## 0. Executive result

Let

\[
C_Q
=
\mathcal T_Q\times\mathcal X_Q\times\mathcal K_Q
\]

be a finite observation carrier with one declared adjacency relation in each
domain.  A real-valued feature candidate is

\[
f:C_Q\longrightarrow\mathbf R.
\]

For a binary characteristic, \(f=\chi_U\) takes values in \(\{0,1\}\).
The directional energies are

\[
E_D(f)
=
\frac12
\sum_{u\sim_D v}
w_D(u,v)
\lvert f(u)-f(v)\rvert^2,
\qquad
D\in\{t,X,K\},
\]

where each unoriented edge is counted according to one fixed convention.  In
the executable cube every edge is enumerated once, so the implementation
omits the redundant factor \(1/2\).

For a binary characteristic, \(E_D\) is precisely the weighted size of the
boundary cut in direction \(D\).  Hence

\[
E_D(\chi_U)=0
\]

if and only if \(U\) is constant on every connected component generated by
the declared \(D\)-moves.

This gives the first clean relation between feature and energy:

\[
\boxed{
\text{exact characteristic along }D
\Longleftrightarrow
\text{zero directional variation along }D.
}
\]

Low but nonzero energy describes an approximate invariant or slow feature,
not an exact characteristic.

# Part I. What “energy” means here

## 1. Four notions that must not be identified

| notion | input | output | required extra structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| directional Dirichlet energy | feature plus weighted adjacency | exact nonnegative variation | graph or local move structure |
| computational resource cost | program execution | steps, memory, communication, or another resource | operational cost model |
| action or dissipation | path through states | accumulated path functional | dynamics and time parameter |
| physical energy | physical state | quantity with units and balance law | physical interpretation, calibration, symmetries |

The finite fixture establishes only the first row.  A checked execution cost
may later calibrate part of temporal or constructive energy, but equality
between them is not assumed.  Physical energy would additionally require
units, a balance or conservation law, and a justified coupling to a physical
model.

Calling every scalar “energy” would hide the main problem rather than solve
it.

## 2. Energy is not contained in a characteristic

A characteristic map

\[
\chi_U:C_Q\to\{0,1\}
\]

does not determine an energy until the allowed local moves and their weights
are specified.  The same subset \(U\) can have a small boundary in one
geometry and a large boundary in another.

Thus the minimum finite energy datum is

\[
(C_Q,
G_t,G_X,G_K,
w_t,w_X,w_K,
f).
\]

The satisfaction form \(\Lambda_Q\) of note 0046 may determine which triples
are positively accepted, but its positive support alone does not supply the
ambient negative or unresolved observations, adjacency, scale, or weights
needed for an energy calculation.

Energy is therefore an enrichment of the 3-form, not a number secretly
already present in it.

## 3. Sierpinski evidence is not yet numeric

The Sierpinski codomain \(\mathbb S\) distinguishes positive evidence from
lack of positive evidence.  It does not supply subtraction, a norm, or a
distance between truth readings.

To compute Dirichlet energy one must choose either:

1. a finite exhaustive Boolean calibration where negative cases are known;
2. a real-valued observable lifting of the positive evidence; or
3. interval-valued energy bounds that retain unresolved cases.

The current test uses the first two in a completely enumerated cube.  It must
not be generalized by silently replacing `Unknown` with zero.

# Part II. Three directional energies

## 4. Temporal variation

Let \(u\sim_t v\) mean that two observations differ by one declared temporal
move while their spatial and constructive coordinates agree.  Then

\[
E_t(f)
=
\sum_{u\sim_t v}
w_t(u,v)
\lvert f(u)-f(v)\rvert^2.
\]

It measures variation of the feature along the temporal adjacency, not
runtime by itself.  A later operational calibration might choose weights from
checked step costs, but the present weights are declared unit weights.

## 5. Spatial variation

Let \(u\sim_X v\) mean that one spatial move changes while temporal and
constructive coordinates agree.  Then

\[
E_X(f)
=
\sum_{u\sim_X v}
w_X(u,v)
\lvert f(u)-f(v)\rvert^2.
\]

For a binary characteristic this is a finite boundary measure.  It makes
precise the intuition that a spatial feature is stable when its truth region
has little boundary relative to the declared observation geometry.

## 6. Constructive variation

Let \(u\sim_K v\) mean that two observations differ by one declared
construction move while time and space agree.  Then

\[
E_K(f)
=
\sum_{u\sim_K v}
w_K(u,v)
\lvert f(u)-f(v)\rvert^2.
\]

This does not equal program length or proof cost.  It measures how a feature
changes when the construction coordinate changes.  Distinct witnesses may
have the same truth value and therefore zero energy for one proposition while
remaining far apart under another construction-sensitive observable.

## 7. Why the vector comes before the scalar

The three directional energies generally have different meanings and units.
The intrinsic first report is

\[
\mathbf E_Q(f)
=
(E_t(f),E_X(f),E_K(f)).
\]

A scalarization requires calibration coefficients

\[
\alpha_Q
=
(\alpha_t,\alpha_X,\alpha_K),
\qquad
\alpha_D>0,
\]

and defines

\[
E_{Q,\alpha}(f)
=
\alpha_tE_t(f)
+
\alpha_XE_X(f)
+
\alpha_KE_K(f).
\]

Changing \(\alpha\) can reverse which of two features is cheaper.  There is no
observer-independent total order before this calibration.

This gives a concrete possible role for the startup calibration of the three
computers: it may include the conversion of their directional scales.  This
is only a structural connection.  No theorem yet identifies startup
calibration with choosing \(\alpha\).

# Part III. The finite cube

## 8. Carrier and edges

The executable fixture uses

\[
\mathcal T_Q
=
\mathcal X_Q
=
\mathcal K_Q
=
\{0,1\},
\]

so

\[
C_Q=\{0,1\}^3.
\]

Two vertices are adjacent in direction \(D\) when only the \(D\)-coordinate
changes.  Each direction has four unit-weight edges.

This cube is not claimed to be a universal 3-form.  It is the smallest carrier
with enough coordinate-wise edges to separate all three directional energies.

## 9. Exact feature table

The fixture computes:

| feature | definition | \(E_t\) | \(E_X\) | \(E_K\) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| constant | \(0\) | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| temporal coordinate | \(t\) | 4 | 0 | 0 |
| spatial coordinate | \(x\) | 0 | 4 | 0 |
| construction coordinate | \(k\) | 0 | 0 | 4 |
| conjunction | \(txk\) | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| majority | \(1[t+x+k\ge2]\) | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| parity | \((t+x+k)\bmod2\) | 4 | 4 | 4 |

The table exposes two facts.

First, a feature can be exactly invariant in two domains and vary entirely in
the third.  Second, coordinated characteristics such as conjunction,
majority, and parity have distinct three-direction energy profiles even though
all are symmetric under coordinate permutation.

## 10. The zero-energy trap

The constant characteristic minimizes every directional energy:

\[
\mathbf E_Q(0)=(0,0,0).
\]

Therefore “learning finds the minimum-energy feature” is vacuous unless a
nontriviality constraint is supplied.  Possible constraints include:

- a fixed positive-region volume;
- fixed boundary observations;
- nonzero variance;
- separation of declared examples; or
- orthogonality to already learned constant or lower modes.

With such a constraint, low-energy features become meaningful cut or slow-mode
problems.  Without it, the theory rewards saying nothing.

# Part IV. Observer forgetting and hidden energy

## 11. Forgetting the construction coordinate

Let

\[
q:
\{0,1\}^3
\longrightarrow
\{0,1\}^2,
\qquad
q(t,x,k)=(t,x),
\]

forget the construction coordinate.

For a real-valued feature \(f\), define the coarse conditional average

\[
\bar f(t,x)
=
\frac12
\sum_{k\in\{0,1\}}
f(t,x,k).
\]

Pull it back to the cube and define the residual

\[
r
=
f-q^*\bar f.
\]

Every construction fibre has zero residual mean:

\[
\sum_k r(t,x,k)=0.
\]

## 12. Induced quotient weights

Each temporal or spatial edge in the quotient represents two parallel fine
edges, one for each construction value.  Its induced weight is therefore two.

With these induced weights, the pulled-back coarse energy equals the quotient
energy.  This prevents an accidental factor-of-two change from being called
physical dissipation.

## 13. Exact energy decomposition

Because the coarse part is constant on each construction fibre and the
residual has zero fibre mean, the cross terms vanish.  The finite cube obeys

\[
E_t(f)
=
E_t(q^*\bar f)
+
E_t(r),
\]

\[
E_X(f)
=
E_X(q^*\bar f)
+
E_X(r),
\]

and

\[
E_K(f)
=
E_K(r),
\qquad
E_K(q^*\bar f)=0.
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\mathbf E_Q(f)
=
\mathbf E_Q(q^*\bar f)
+
\mathbf E_Q(r).
}
\]

The executable fixture checks the identity with exact rational arithmetic for
a nontrivial polynomial feature, not only for the symmetric binary examples.

This equality depends on the product cube, uniform fibre average, and induced
weights.  A general observer quotient may have cross terms or no canonical
average.  The general output must then be a comparison report, not a claimed
conservation law.

## 14. Descent criterion

A feature descends exactly through \(q\) when it is constant on every
construction fibre:

\[
f=q^*g
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
E_K(f)=0
\]

for this two-point connected fibre.

This strengthens the previous logical quotient language.  A coarse
proposition is not merely an image of fine truth values; exact descent means
that construction variation carries no hidden energy for the selected
feature.

## 15. Parity becomes featureless after averaging

For

\[
p(t,x,k)
=(t+x+k)\bmod2,
\]

the conditional average is

\[
\bar p(t,x)=\frac12
\]

at every coarse point.  Hence

\[
\mathbf E( q^*\bar p )=(0,0,0),
\]

while

\[
\mathbf E(p)=(4,4,4).
\]

All variation lies in the residual.

If the coarse observer forces \(1/2\) to either false or true, it obtains a
constant Boolean proposition and sees no energy.  This is a precise finite
example of how Booleanization can make an actively varying fine feature appear
institutionally settled.

# Part V. Energy, logic, and learning

## 16. What logic forgets about energy

The support order of note 0044 records

\[
A\models_Q B
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{supp}(A)
\subseteq
\operatorname{supp}(B).
\]

It does not record:

- the boundary energy of either support;
- the cost of transporting evidence from \(A\) to \(B\);
- the temporal dissipation of proof search;
- the constructive work of maintaining a witness; or
- hidden energy inside an observer fibre.

An energy-enriched proposition would therefore need at least

\[
(A,
\mathbf E_Q(A),
R_Q^E(A)),
\]

where the residual records energy invisible to the coarse proposition.  The
triple is a research notation, not a stable semantic type.

## 17. Stability is stronger than having a characteristic

Finding a characteristic does not by itself make a system dynamically stable.
Stability requires a declared dynamics and a statement about perturbations,
barriers, or return.

The energy view suggests three increasingly strong claims:

1. **invariance:** \(E_D(f)=0\) along selected moves;
2. **low variation:** \(E_D(f)\) is small under a declared normalization;
3. **dynamical stability:** a declared evolution returns toward a basin or
   cannot cross its boundary below a stated energy barrier.

Only the first two are calibrated here.  Calling a learned proposition an
institution or a stable regime requires the third layer or an explicit social
analogue.

## 18. A possible generated direction

Once a metric on feature space and a scalar calibration \(\alpha\) have been
chosen, the energy can generate a relaxation direction:

\[
\frac{df}{ds}
=
-\nabla E_{Q,\alpha}(f).
\]

On a finite graph this is a heat-flow or diffusion-like proposal.  Low modes
change slowly and can appear as learned features.

This direction is not canonical before:

- the admissible feature space is fixed;
- the nontriviality constraint is fixed;
- the three energy scales are calibrated;
- boundary forcing is declared; and
- the observer treatment of unresolved values is specified.

Moreover, exploration and novelty may require energy injection, barrier
crossing, or a change of observer rather than monotone relaxation.  The
gradient proposal explains stabilization; it does not explain the whole of
learning or life.

## 19. Energy and entropy remain separate

Observer fibres also have multiplicity.  With a measure, that multiplicity
may support an entropy.  Energy and entropy are not the same object:

\[
E
\ne
S.
\]

A free-energy expression

\[
F=E-\tau S
\]

requires a measure, a temperature-like scale \(\tau\), and an ensemble or
sampling interpretation.  None is supplied by the present cube.  The exact
energy residual is a prerequisite for that later question, not already a
thermodynamic theory.

# Part VI. Relation to the larger philosophical question

## 20. The narrow implication

A characteristic makes many events equivalent.  Logic can stabilize that
equivalence into rules and institutions.  The energy layer adds what the
settled characteristic alone cannot express:

- how much variation remains inside the equivalence;
- how much tension lies at its boundary;
- what is required to maintain the regime; and
- which hidden directions could reopen it.

This does not answer the larger question of novelty, reverence, curiosity,
adventure, or the many possible paths between emptiness and universality.  It
does prevent one premature philosophical conclusion: a stable characteristic
is not the end of motion.  It may be only a low-energy projection whose
residual still contains the capacity for change.

The rest of that question should not be forced into the present finite energy
calibration.

# Part VII. Boundaries and next tests

## 21. What the calibration establishes

Within the declared finite cube, the fixture establishes:

1. exact temporal, spatial, and constructive edge sets;
2. exact rational directional Dirichlet energies;
3. the energy profiles of seven finite characteristics;
4. the zero-energy constant-feature counterexample;
5. the necessity of explicit scalar calibration weights;
6. exact conditional averaging along the construction fibre;
7. exact coarse-plus-residual energy decomposition;
8. exact descent when constructive energy vanishes; and
9. complete hiding of parity energy by coarse averaging.

## 22. What it does not establish

The calibration does not establish:

- physical energy or conservation;
- equality between graph energy and execution cost;
- a canonical metric or adjacency for Adva programs;
- a canonical scalar sum of three directional energies;
- a general quotient-energy theorem;
- a learning dynamics or convergence theorem;
- a spectral feature theorem for open observers;
- a relation between energy and Chaitin \(\Omega\);
- a stable institution model;
- a free-energy or thermodynamic semantics; or
- a resolution of the larger philosophical problem.

## 23. Next decisive experiments

The next energy work should proceed only through explicit calibrations.

1. Replace unit temporal weights by checked finite execution costs and test
   whether the directional order changes.
2. Give one Adva construction family an explicit adjacency derived from
   certified rewrites rather than host-defined cube edges.
3. Test a non-product observer quotient where the orthogonal decomposition
   fails or produces a cross residual.
4. Compare a section-generated logical characteristic with the lowest
   nonconstant binary-energy characteristic under the same boundary
   constraint.
5. Determine whether construction-history residuals can change a later
   extensional energy rather than remain audit-only.
6. Only after a measure and ensemble are declared, ask whether entropy and
   free energy add explanatory value.

## 24. Conservative conclusion

The clean energy statement is:

> A learned characteristic becomes an energetic object only after the
> observer supplies typed local moves and scales.  Its first invariant is a
> three-direction energy vector.  Observer forgetting may hide energy inside
> its fibres, so a stable coarse feature must be reported together with an
> exact energetic residual whenever possible.

This adds dynamics and tension to characteristic inference without pretending
that every computational variation is already physical energy.
