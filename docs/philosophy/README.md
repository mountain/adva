# Philosophy Notes

Status: historical and philosophical research context for Adva. These notes do
not define stable semantics, register executable claims, or promote research
objects into the Rust authority boundary.

This directory preserves intellectual sources and the path by which they
change the questions asked of the project. It keeps four layers distinct:

1. **historical report** -- what a cited thinker or text actually proposed;
2. **Adva interpretation** -- how that material illuminates current research;
3. **working hypothesis** -- a new construction that remains unproved; and
4. **bounded evidence** -- what an existing fixture or certificate supports.

No historical analogy is evidence that an Adva mechanism is correct. Conversely,
a failed formalization may identify a genuine philosophical obstruction worth
preserving.

## Provenance

This line of inquiry was initiated by Mingli Yuan while examining the essential
difficulty of finite empirical learning. The decisive questions were:

- how can a finite learner exceed the language fixed by its experience;
- what formal role corresponds to human imagination;
- whether the beginning of a process is itself an emptiness or hole;
- whether **opening a hole** and **closing a hole** are more primitive than
  crossing an already-given singularity; and
- why the complete exploration history must remain available, since a later
  observer may use it to open a new hole.

The notes below reconstruct historical sources and red-team the resulting Adva
synthesis. They do not claim that Leibniz anticipated Adva, the triadic
computer, program geometry, or the proposed open logic.

## Editorial discipline

Future notes in this directory should:

- cite primary texts where practical and distinguish them from later
  reconstruction;
- record the motivating intuition, supporting evidence, conservative
  conclusion, red-team objections, and falsification handles;
- retain failed routes and changes of vocabulary rather than rewriting the
  history as a straight path;
- distinguish comparison from identification and abstraction from a right to
  forget; and
- link any engineering proposal to exact fixtures, negative controls, result
  forms, and promotion boundaries.

Executable claims continue to belong in `docs/claims.toml`. Semantic decisions
continue to require an ADR and the authority rules in `AGENTS.md`.
