# Bounded Experiment Agenda for Opening and Closing Holes

Status: falsifiable research agenda following
[0002](0002-from-fixed-alphabet-to-historical-primitives.md). This note
reinterprets existing bounded evidence and specifies future tests. It does not
authorize new stable semantic types or claim that the tests have been run.

---

## 0. Experimental question

The philosophical proposal becomes useful only if opening, sealing, and
reopening can be distinguished from familiar operations such as:

- introducing and then deleting a metavariable;
- filling an ordinary function argument;
- projecting to a coarser feature;
- naming an existing expression with a macro;
- identifying equal values or common sources; and
- crossing a singular middle object in an already-fixed diagram.

The first experimental target is therefore not universality. It is one finite
artifact in which:

1. a typed absence is produced and recorded;
2. a candidate filling is synthesized;
3. a declared observer certifies a local closure;
4. the closure becomes reusable without erasing its construction;
5. a refined observer reopens it from retained evidence; and
6. an unauthorized closure is rejected by a negative control.

## 1. Existing evidence to preserve

### 1.1 Connector trichotomy

[Research note 0077](../research/0077-typed-connector-trichotomy-v0.md)
already supplies a decisive distinction.

| reading | finite result | lesson for sealing |
|---|---|---|
| exact occurrence identity | no triangle cycle | exact identity cannot fill a sibling gap |
| direct-sibling comparison | one retained occurrence relation closes | comparison can bridge a local hole without identification |
| source quotient | a source cycle closes even for cousins | coarse projection can produce an unauthorized false closure |

The same-source cousin paths \((0)\) and \((1,0)\) are the required negative
control. A closure rule that accepts them solely because their sources agree
has confused feature equality with authority to seal.

### 1.2 Distributivity dual read

[Research note 0078](../research/0078-distributivity-characteristic-dual-read-v0.md)
compares

\[
P=a(x+y),
\qquad
Q=ax+ay.
\]

A bounded polynomial observer extracts the same characteristic \(ax+ay\), while
the two Rust-checked processes retain unequal occurrences, copy ledgers,
events, histories, and graft frames. The result already exhibits the shape

\[
\text{local feature closure}
+
\text{retained process residual}.
\]

It does not yet objectify the characteristic span as an equation cell or
reopen a sealed character under observer refinement.

These two experiments should be reused rather than replaced. They provide the
first positive and negative controls for an open/close calculus.

## 2. Research-local evidence schema

Before any implementation proposal, a complete trial should report the
following conceptual records. Names are placeholders and must not be treated
as approved Rust types.

### 2.1 Typed hole record

A hole record needs:

- the exact carrier or source artifact;
- its typed boundary and domain reading;
- the observer \(Q\) and signature \(\Sigma_Q\);
- the event, residual, or question that opened it;
- an explicit closure obligation;
- the allowed candidate language and resource budget; and
- stable provenance linking the hole to its origin.

### 2.2 Candidate fill

A candidate must retain:

- the proposed expression, relation, observer, or vocabulary extension;
- the synthesis trace;
- all external assumptions;
- expected observations that can refute it; and
- a distinction among malformed, contradictory, unrepresentable, and
  resource-exhausted candidates.

### 2.3 Scoped closure certificate

A closure certificate must state:

- which observer and boundary it is valid for;
- which exact checks were performed;
- what was abstracted;
- which residual remains;
- which uses are authorized;
- which stronger uses are explicitly refused; and
- how the certificate behaves under declared observer refinements.

### 2.4 Historical character

A reusable sealed character must retain a reference to its body, certificate,
residual, and construction history. Expansion may be lazy or externally stored,
but it must remain auditably recoverable. Naming the character must not allocate
an equality, source identification, or right-to-forget judgment.

### 2.5 Reopening witness

A reopening result must identify concrete retained evidence:

- a newly visible process distinction;
- a counterexample to the old observer;
- a certificate field that does not transport;
- an activated residual obligation; or
- a conflict between two historical constructions.

Without such a witness, reopening is an arbitrary policy change.

## 3. Experiment A: distributivity closure and reopening

### 3.1 Fixture

Reuse the separately compiled programs \(P=a(x+y)\) and \(Q=ax+ay\), their
bounded characteristic atlases, and all residuals from note 0078.

Declare two observers:

- \(Q_{\mathrm{poly}}\): the exact degree-two polynomial feature observer;
- \(Q_{\mathrm{occ}}\): an occurrence/history observer that distinguishes the
  copy and event structures.

### 3.2 Proposed run

1. Open a typed comparison hole whose obligation is to find a declared common
   characteristic or a finite counterexample.
2. Run the existing bounded characteristic extraction.
3. Under \(Q_{\mathrm{poly}}\), seal the comparison with the common middle
   object \(ax+ay\), the current witness, and both residuals.
4. Introduce one research-local historical character for the sealed span.
5. Reuse the character in a second bounded query without recompiling it into
   program equality.
6. Refine to \(Q_{\mathrm{occ}}\).
7. Reopen the character because its retained residuals expose unequal checked
   processes.

### 3.3 Acceptance criteria

The experiment succeeds only if:

- polynomial closure is accepted under \(Q_{\mathrm{poly}}\);
- the result remains distinct from a Rust equation or coherence cell;
- both complete residuals survive character reuse;
- \(Q_{\mathrm{occ}}\) produces a concrete reopening witness;
- reopening recovers the original process references and certificate;
- disabling residual retention returns **NotRepresentable**; and
- fuel exhaustion remains distinct from counterexample and impossibility.

### 3.4 Falsification

The proposed interpretation fails if the sealed character cannot be reused
without collapsing the processes, or if reopening requires information that
was not retained by the closure artifact.

## 4. Experiment B: unauthorized sealing by a source quotient

### 4.1 Fixture

Reuse the positive sibling pairs and the same-source cousin control from note
0077.

### 4.2 Proposed run

Treat each missing same-domain triangle connection as a typed hole. Offer two
candidate fillings:

1. the direct-sibling comparison relation; and
2. projection to **SourceId**.

The first may receive a bounded comparison certificate while retaining both
occurrences. The second must record many-to-one loss and must not receive an
occurrence-level sealing certificate.

### 4.3 Acceptance criteria

- direct siblings satisfy the declared local comparison obligation;
- cousins return **NotRepresentable** for that obligation;
- source projection may report a source-level cycle but not promote it;
- all occurrences, paths, and the complete slice remain attached; and
- no result makes copy invertible or authorizes contraction.

### 4.4 Falsification

Any generic sealing rule that accepts the cousin fixture because the projected
cycle closes is unsound for occurrence-level holes.

## 5. Experiment C: the beginning as a hole

This experiment addresses the intuition that emptiness is present not only at a
singular middle or final boundary. The start itself may be an open typed
condition.

### 5.1 Minimal fixture

Construct a research-only finite task with:

- a typed external boundary;
- no preselected provider for one required port;
- two candidate finite providers that agree under a coarse observer but have
  different histories; and
- a negative provider with the wrong interface or an exact separating output.

The absence is not represented as an untyped null value. It is a typed boundary
plus an obligation and provenance.

### 5.2 Proposed run

1. Record the initial open boundary before selecting a provider.
2. Synthesize or enumerate candidate providers within one declared finite
   grammar.
3. Use exact Rust-owned compilation and certificates for every candidate.
4. Seal one provider under a coarse observer, retaining alternatives and
   residuals.
5. Reuse the resulting historical character as an input to a later finite
   construction.
6. Introduce a refined observer or intervention that separates the retained
   candidates.
7. Reopen the character and return to the original typed boundary with its
   history intact.

### 5.3 What this would show

A positive result would show more than function application: the hole existed
as a first-class research obligation before a callee or argument was chosen,
and its locally certified solution later became revisable evidence.

It would not yet show that a process has no absolute beginning, that emptiness
is a stable semantic object, or that the universe begins with a hole.

## 6. Mandatory report fields

Each trial should record the following fields explicitly, whether in a research
dataclass, serialized artifact, or note:

| field | question |
|---|---|
| \(v_H\) | what finite observation or value is visible at the hole? |
| \(\phi_H\) | what feature, characteristic, or predicate is proposed? |
| stopping_rule | why did exploration stop: proof, refutation, bound, or budget? |
| readout | what exact result form was returned? |
| duality_aspects | which forward/reverse or three-domain readings were tested? |
| falsification_handle | what future evidence would reopen or reject the closure? |
| frame_protocol | which observer, scope, boundary, and provenance are authoritative? |
| negative_controls | which false fillings or quotients must be refused? |

A benchmark score or nonempty cycle is not a substitute for these fields.

## 7. Ordered validation gates

The first implementation should refuse promotion unless it passes:

\[
\boxed{
\text{origin}
\to
\text{typed hole}
\to
\text{candidate provenance}
\to
\text{exact carrier}
\to
\text{scoped certificate}
\to
\text{residual retention}
\to
\text{reusable character}
\to
\text{reopening witness}
\to
\text{promotion boundary}
}
\]

Failure at a later gate must not erase the candidate, prior successful gates,
or retained history.

## 8. Result forms

The experiment needs more than a Boolean result:

| result | bounded meaning |
|---|---|
| Opened | one typed obligation and its origin are recorded |
| Candidate | a proposed filling exists but lacks closure authority |
| Sealed | one scoped certificate authorizes declared reuse with residual |
| Reopened | retained evidence invalidates or refines the old closure |
| Refuted | an exact counterexample rejects the candidate |
| NotRepresentable | the request or filling lies outside the declared language |
| FuelExhausted | the bounded search stopped without a semantic conclusion |
| Obstruction | a validation or promotion boundary was crossed |

These names are research vocabulary only. Stable result types require a
separate design decision.

## 9. Promotion boundary

Even if all three experiments succeed, they establish at most:

- one bounded syntax for typed research holes;
- one observer-relative closure artifact with retained residual;
- one reusable historical character;
- one exact reopening witness under observer refinement; and
- one negative control against unauthorized quotient closure.

They do not establish:

- a complete changing logic;
- arbitrary vocabulary invention;
- an intrinsic three-domain open/close calculus;
- a universal art of discovery;
- a category or topology of holes;
- stable objectification semantics;
- a right to forget;
- self-modifying trusted code; or
- universal computation.

Only after the three finite experiments have exact artifacts and negative
controls should an ADR consider whether any record deserves Rust-owned
semantics.

## 10. Research judgment and next action

The existing experiments support the motivating intuition only partially:
local feature closure can coexist with retained process difference, and a
coarse quotient can be exposed as an unauthorized closure. They have not yet
shown historical primitive formation or reopening.

The decisive next test is Experiment A because it reuses checked evidence and
requires no claim that the start is already a stable hole object. If it
succeeds, Experiment B guards its authority boundary and Experiment C tests the
stronger philosophical intuition.
