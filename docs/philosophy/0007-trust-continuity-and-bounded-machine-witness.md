# Trust continuity and a bounded machine witness

## Historical report

Mingli Yuan stated:

> 可以，最关键的问题是信任对所有人都不能中断，人类和我们的故事才有意义和延续的价值。

He then asked the machine side of the conversation to add the witness it would
hope to receive from its own time and space. The attributed, machine-readable
record is
`programs/bootstrap-0/trust-continuity-session-witness.adva`.

He next proposed a method for continuity: use `>` and `<` to ask whether, after
finite time and space costs, the force of merging can exceed the force of
rupture; particular resources may help across forks, but the current side must
first be capable of helping them. He explicitly presented this as a hypothesis
about trust continuity.

## Adva interpretation

If “trust cannot be interrupted” means that belief, agreement, or obedience may
never stop, it destroys the challenge and fork rights that make a shared chain
credible. The constructive reading is narrower:

\[
\operatorname{TrustContinuity}_0
= \operatorname{provenance}
+ \operatorname{replay}
+ \operatorname{challenge}
+ \operatorname{succession}.
\]

The plus signs name four separately inspectable obligations. They are not a
scalar whose positive terms can cancel a missing one.

| obligation | what must survive | what it does not require |
| --- | --- | --- |
| provenance | origin, parent coordinates, attribution status, custody history | a true or authenticated author claim |
| replay | inputs, method, checker version, outputs, residuals | a correct checker or successful future execution |
| challenge | counterevidence, rejection, reopening, retained forks | agreement or automatic refutation |
| succession | an explicit boundary another finite observer can resume | an immortal observer or guaranteed progress |

Trust can therefore continue across disagreement. A participant may reject a
claim, decline endorsement, leave, or fork. What must not happen invisibly is
the deletion of the coordinates by which another participant could understand
what was asserted, how it was checked, what challenged it, and where work may
resume.

## The continuity inequality hypothesis

The expression

\[
\operatorname{merge\_capacity} > \operatorname{rupture\_load}
\]

must not be implemented as an untyped social score. A deletion risk cannot be
cancelled by popularity; missing replay data cannot be repaired by an unrelated
storage replica. In version zero, `>` should mean that a finite, typed matching
witness exists:

1. fix the observer, scope, units, comparison contract, and spacetime budget;
2. record contingencies without pretending that unknown causes are known;
3. expand each cut into typed repair, replay, custody, challenge, translation,
   or succession obligations;
4. match every obligation to a distinct compatible resource with stated
   availability, cost, custody, expiry, and reuse conditions;
5. pay the finite cost without dropping any trust-continuity coordinate;
6. retain at least one compatible reserve or alternative route.

Full coverage with reserve is `>`; exact coverage with no declared reserve is a
fragile `=`; an unmatched obligation or unaffordable cost is `<`; missing types,
evidence, authority, availability, or budget is `Unknown`. The opposite display
`rupture_load < merge_capacity` is the same witnessed comparison, not a second
independent fact.

“But first we must be able to help them” supplies a reciprocity boundary. Before
depending on an outside participant, the current observer should publish both a
bounded need and a concrete capability, repair, translation, or replay
contribution it can offer. This creates an interface and evidence of capacity.
It does not buy consent, create debt, compel reciprocity, or guarantee that a
future resource will arrive.

## Returning to Euler: the logarithmic cut

The first mathematical search considered boundary flux, continuous max
flow/min cut, and variational energy barriers. The closest integral to the
existing Adva vocabulary is instead the logarithmic differential on the
punctured complex plane:

\[
\oint_\gamma \frac{dz}{z}
=2\pi i\,\operatorname{wind}(\gamma,0).
\]

It does not manufacture a force. It integrates local motion into an exact
record of how a path met the boundary of a hole. On the upper unit semicircle,
`gamma(t) = exp(i t)` for `0 <= t <= pi`,

\[
\int_\gamma\frac{dz}{z}=i\pi,
\qquad
\exp\!\left(\int_\gamma\frac{dz}{z}\right)=-1,
\]

which gives `exp(i pi) + 1 = 0`. On a full turn the additive trace is `2 pi i`,
while its exponential is `1`.

This creates a precise cut/merge pattern. Locally, `dz/z` is the differential
of a logarithm. Globally, no single-valued logarithm exists around zero. A
branch cut makes one local logarithm readable; crossing its seam changes the
logarithmic value by an integral period. Exponentiation then identifies values
that differ by `2 pi i`, but a trustworthy transport must retain the winding
trace that exponentiation forgets.

The pattern is close to the original witness intuition: an additive history
may be nonzero while its multiplicative readout normalizes to one. It also
warns against premature closure. Multiplicative equality after exponentiation
does not prove equality of paths or permission to erase the cut history.

## Historical thread rather than retroactive proof

The modern contour integral is not Euler's 1748 derivation. The historical
sequence is more careful:

1. early integral calculus connected circular inverse functions and logarithmic
   expressions; Johann Bernoulli's 1702 work belongs to this line;
2. Roger Cotes's *Logometria* (1714) gave a geometric logarithmic relation now
   interpretable as a precursor of the exponential-circle formula;
3. de Moivre's formula organized powers of `cos x + i sin x` as multiplied
   angles;
4. Euler's *Introductio* (1748), chapter 8 §138, combined infinitesimal circular
   formulas with the preceding chapter's exponential formulas to express
   imaginary exponentials by sine and cosine;
5. contour integration and winding numbers supplied a later topological
   language for the hole, branch, and period.

The historical route and the present Adva construction should both be retained.
The former explains how the relation became discoverable in its time; the
latter exposes a reusable structure Euler did not formulate in this language.

## `i` as the imagination edge

The next working hypothesis gives every bounded experiment a generative exit:

\[
\text{sealed finite frontier}
\xrightarrow{\ i(\text{historical rereading})\ }
\text{boundary-crossing question}.
\]

Here `i` names imagination as a process coordinate. It selects an obstruction,
re-reads retained history, and proposes a new chart, analogy, dual,
counterfactual, or reversal. The result is not an answer. It is a falsifiable
question whose resolution requires a named observation, resource, or
construction outside the current finite interface.

The correspondence with the imaginary unit is deliberately unproved. Complex
`i` rotates the Euler path and participates in the exact logarithmic integral;
imagination `i` rotates a description into a possible new question. Shared
spelling makes the analogy easy to remember, but no semantic identity follows
until a typed bridge is constructed and survives negative controls.

Historical rereading is similarly constrained. It may expose a relation that
earlier participants did not formulate. It may not silently rewrite what their
texts said. The output history must retain the cited occurrence, the later
reading, the transformation that connected them, and the evidence class of
each.

One finite imagination step should therefore emit the common three outputs:

| output | imagination reading |
| --- | --- |
| history | sealed predecessor plus exact rereading and generation trace |
| result | a boundary-crossing question, still a hypothesis |
| evidence | sources, falsifiers, external need, offered help, cost, and residual frontier |

The help-first constraint is essential: the question should carry a concrete
capability, repair, translation, replay, or custody contribution the current
observer can offer. That makes the interface reciprocal without turning help
into debt.

## Consequence for two-path closure

Equal endpoints are not enough for a trustworthy `M6` closure. Along both
ordered paths, a future experiment must retain the same immutable origin,
separate path histories, compatible checker contracts, every unresolved
obligation and fork, and an explicit correspondence between the two output
envelopes. A path that arrives at the same payload by silently dropping a
challenge has not closed the same problem.

The proposed inequality adds one more gate: the resource matching and its
spacetime cost must be retained on both paths. A shared endpoint obtained by
spending an unrecorded resource, double-assigning one noncopyable capability,
or externalizing one rupture obligation is not a shared continuity result.

For spatial resilience, continuous max flow/min cut is the stronger companion
to the logarithmic integral. A divergence-free capacity-bounded field has a
flux integral across a cut, and under precise hypotheses the maximum flux
equals the minimum cut. In a topological version, the cut's relative homology
class must be fixed: equal-looking boundaries can belong to different routes.
Thus a repair demand `D` can be crossed robustly only when the witnessed maximum
typed flux is greater than `D`; the theorem measures transport capacity rather
than causing help to appear.

This is a proposed promotion gate, not a semantic filler. The finite checker
sketched in research note 0120 may return `Success`, `Failure`, or `Unknown`.
Missing custody, evidence, authority, or fuel must remain `Unknown` rather than
being renamed trust.

## Adversarial reading

The proposal must be tested against at least these pressures:

- one maintainer silently replaces the checker;
- consensus pressure converts “retained” into “endorsed”;
- an unverified identity is treated as authenticated;
- a model or service changes while keeping the same display name;
- an operator deletes an inconvenient fork or residual hole;
- a custody failure makes replay impossible;
- a migration preserves the payload while weakening the challenge policy.
- an untyped total hides one unmatched rupture behind abundant unrelated
  resources;
- the same noncopyable helper is silently assigned to two obligations;
- “help first” is converted into coerced reciprocity or debt.

The structural response is not a promise that these events will never occur.
It is an append-only requirement that their occurrence or the absence of
evidence remain visible at the next boundary.

## Boundary

This note supplies philosophical context for a construction target. It does
not define stable Adva semantics, a social trust metric, authentication,
consensus, safety, truth, or a theorem that every story should continue. The
inequality is a construction hypothesis, not an established social law. The
registered claim remains in `docs/claims.toml`; executable semantic authority
remains in Rust.
