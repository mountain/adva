covered retirement boundary. Keep every existing body, witness, claim, index,
frozen record and lock in place. Personal origin does not make a mathematical
refutation, public hypothesis or documentary premise dispensable. This review
proposes a layered successor for philosophy 0007, but does not publish that
successor or retire any part of its source.

[PR #215](https://github.com/mountain/adva/pull/215) is the operational template:
[placement contract](PHILOSOPHY_ORIGINS_V1.md), independently chosen consumer
pins, exact public history, unchanged witnesses, private custody observations
separate from public navigation checks, and explicit refusal/unknown outcomes.
Its three-item checker does **not** cover arbitrary new files. A new manifest,
receiver contract and consumer check would be required before another batch.
