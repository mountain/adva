# Research proposal journals, not knowledge epochs

This directory is separate from `../stability/`. An exploration journal may
retain rejected proposals, Open and Unknown outcomes. None of those files is
admitted by the knowledge-epoch loader.

Research 0151 uses `library_generation` to record three bounded generation,
ablation and macro-control stages under `0151/`. A reusable proposal book is
reconstructed only by checking the base snapshot, regenerating the complete
prior stages and comparing every field. A saved successful recipe is still
proposal syntax: its future expanded pairs must be checked anew.

The original two knowledge snapshots and their witness content remain
unchanged. No stable Lisp operation, native program identity, all-fillings
closure or general vocabulary promotion belongs to this journal.

Execution status and replay commands are retained in
[Research 0151 evidence](../../docs/research/0151-evidence/README.md).
