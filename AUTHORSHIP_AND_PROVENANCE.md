# Authorship and provenance

This file records research origin, implementation authorship, review state and
human disposition separately. A GitHub account, commit author field, merge, CI
success or citation entry does not by itself establish personal authorship,
independent review or mathematical correctness. See
[`docs/AI_ATTRIBUTION.md`](docs/AI_ATTRIBUTION.md) for the account-proxy rule.

Status vocabulary:

- `merged-bounded-result`: accepted into `main` only within its stated finite scope.
- `draft-bounded-result`: retained on an unmerged research branch.
- `open-direction`: a research question or intuition, not an established claim.
- `rejected-or-unknown`: an executed failure, counterexample or unexecuted route.
